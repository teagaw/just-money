# Risograph — 25 Prompts

Grainy 2-color limited palette prints. Indie zine, band poster, art school aesthetic. The duotone registration-misalignment look is the whole vibe. Use `--style raw` and lower `--s` (200-400). Pair with `--no gradients` to keep the flat riso look. Each Output describes the print you'd expect — paper, ink, registration drift.

**Top picks:** #3 indie gig poster (most useful for sellers), #12 jazz poster (most printable), #19 synthwave event poster (most viral).

---

### Prompt #1
**Prompt:** risograph print of a single daisy, two-color limited palette of mustard yellow and deep blue, halftone dot pattern, slight registration misalignment, off-white paper texture, hand-pulled print look
**Parameters:** `--ar 1:1 --style raw --s 250 --v 6`
**Output:** A square riso print on warm off-white paper, a single daisy in mustard yellow with deep blue underlayer, halftone dots, slight color offset on the petals, hand-pulled texture visible; printed at 300 dpi, no time of day, mustard-and-cobalt palette.
**Pro Tip:** Swap "mustard yellow and deep blue" for "coral pink and forest green" or "fluorescent orange and black" for different color moods.
