# Brutalist — 25 Prompts

Massive concrete, monolithic forms, harsh shadows, and the "build to last 200 years" aesthetic. But the vocabulary is bigger than overcast skies and lonely figures. This file rotates through dawn, dusk, storm, fog, snow, neon, glass, steel, and crowds — not just empty concrete. Use `--s 300-500`, `--ar 3:2` for landscape or `--ar 2:3` for portrait architecture. Pair with `--style raw` for sharper geometric lines.

**Top picks:** #6 flooded courtyard (most original), #17 clock tower at dusk (most cinematic), #25 fog viaduct (most viral).

---
