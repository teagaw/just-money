# Vaporwave — 25 Prompts

80s/90s nostalgia. Neon pink, cyan, purple. Greek statues, palm trees, chrome, grid floors, glitchy text. Aesthetic dream. Use `--s 500-750` for max vibe. `--ar 16:9` and `--ar 21:9` work great. Each Output describes the shot you should expect, with palette and time of day called out.

**Top picks:** #11 floating Greek temple (most surreal), #20 neon highway (most cinematic), #22 cherry blossom grid (most viral).
