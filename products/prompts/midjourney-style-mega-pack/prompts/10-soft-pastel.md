# Soft Pastel — 25 Prompts

Dreamy pinks, baby blues, lavender, cream. But the file is more than Ghibli and cherry blossoms — it rotates through eight sub-styles (noir pastel, cyberpunk pastel, brutalist pastel, vaporwave pastel, rococo, art deco, mid-century, bauhaus) plus quieter slice-of-life moments. Use `--style raw`, `--s 400-600`, soft and ethereal.

**Top picks:** #1 noir detective (most original), #4 cyberpunk pastel (most viral), #6 rococo ballroom (most luxurious).

---

### Prompt #1
**Prompt:** a soft pastel film noir detective, trench coat and fedora in dusty rose, leaning against a lamppost in a blue-hour alley, single sodium light overhead, rain-slicked pastel cobblestones, soft mist, mystery without menace
**Parameters:** `--ar 2:3 --style raw --s 500 --v 6`
**Output:** A vertical composition of a detective in dusty rose trench coat and fedora under a single sodium lamp, blue-hour alley in soft pastels, mist, rain-slick cobblestones; mysterious and tender, evening palette of rose, blue, and soft amber.
