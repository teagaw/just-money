# Anime — 25 Prompts

Authentic Japanese anime/manga style. Use `--niji 6` for the proper look. `--ar 2:3` for character portraits, `--ar 16:9` for scenes, `--ar 1:1` for icons. `--s 300-500` is the sweet spot. Each Output describes the shot you should expect, with palette and time of day called out.

**Top picks:** #5 mecha pilot (most cinematic), #19 floating island battle (most epic), #22 ghost girl (most viral).
