# Isometric — 25 Prompts

30° game-engine angle. Clean lines, no perspective distortion. Rooms, vehicles, products, characters, food, machinery, tools — anything that benefits from the "diorama" or "tiny world" framing. Perfect for game devs, infographic illustrators, and brand assets. Use `--ar 1:1` for square scenes, `--s 250-400`, and rotate between 4–5 different style anchors to keep the file from feeling like a Mad Libs.

**Top picks:** #6 floating sky island (most viral), #11 witch's kitchen (most charming), #22 car repair garage (most original).

---
