# Watercolor — 25 Prompts

Soft washes, paper texture, ink line work, wet-on-wet bleeding. Botanical illustration is part of it, but the file spans cityscape, ocean, mountain, portrait, food, architecture, and abstract work too. Use `--style raw` and `--s 250-400` for the most authentic watercolor look. Pair with `--no gradients` if the bleed is too soft.

**Top picks:** #2 weathered portrait (most original), #4 stormy lighthouse (most cinematic), #19 autumn leaves abstract (most print-ready).

---

### Prompt #1
**Prompt:** loose watercolor of misty mountain peaks at dawn, soft blue and lavender washes, distant fog rolling through valleys, ink line work for foreground pines, wet-on-wet sky bleeding into the peaks, paper texture visible, traditional Chinese landscape influence
**Parameters:** `--ar 16:9 --style raw --s 350 --v 6`
**Output:** A wide watercolor mountain panorama, layered peaks in soft blue and lavender, fog pooling in valleys, ink pines in the foreground, wet-on-wet sky bleeding down; dawn palette of cobalt, lavender, and warm cream.
**Pro Tip:** Add "with a single small temple on a peak" for narrative. Change "dawn" to "storm" for darker palette.
