# Cinematic — 25 Prompts

Film stills, dramatic lighting, movie poster vibes. Use `--s 500-750` for cinematic polish. Most prompts work in `--ar 21:9` (cinematic widescreen) or `--ar 2:3` (poster portrait). Each Output below describes the *shot* you'd expect, with the camera, time of day, and palette called out.

**Top picks:** #1 Mars astronaut (hero), #3 Neo-Tokyo hacker (ad creative), #25 snowy carriage (book cover).

---

### Prompt #1
**Prompt:** a lone astronaut standing on the surface of Mars, dramatic golden hour lighting casting long shadows, dust particles suspended in the thin atmosphere, anamorphic lens flare from the sun, shot on 35mm film, cinematic film still
**Parameters:** `--ar 21:9 --s 500 --v 6`
**Output:** A wide hero shot of a single astronaut on rust-red Martian terrain, golden light raking across the suit and visor, dust motes suspended, vast empty horizon; shot on 35mm anamorphic, late afternoon, rust-and-gold palette.
**Pro Tip:** Add "Earth visible as a tiny blue dot in the sky" for emotional weight. Lower --s to 250 for a more documentary feel.
