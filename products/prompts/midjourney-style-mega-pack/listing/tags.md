## Tag Strategy Notes

**Tag 1-2:** Exact-match product category. Anyone searching "midjourney prompts" or "midjourney prompt pack" should find this.

**Tag 3-4:** Slightly broader. "ai art prompts" captures buyers who don't know Midjourney specifically. "midjourney v6" captures version-specific searchers.

**Tag 5-7:** General digital art/category tags. These have higher competition but are the search path for browsing shoppers.

**Tag 8-9:** Style/format-focused. Buyers who want organized prompt packs.

**Tag 10-11:** Professional positioning. Buyers searching for tools, not just any prompt.

**Tag 12-13:** Use case tags. POD sellers and digital product buyers.
