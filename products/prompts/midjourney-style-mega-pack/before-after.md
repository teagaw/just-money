# Before / After — 10 Examples of Weak vs. Strong Prompts

See the difference a few extra words makes. The "Before" prompts are what most beginners type. The "After" prompts are what you'll find in this pack.

---

## Example 1: Cinematic Astronaut

**❌ Before (generic):**
```
astronaut on mars --v 6
```

**Problems:**
- No setting details
- No lighting
- No mood
- No aspect ratio for "cinematic" feel
- Just one noun, no adjectives

**✅ After (our Cinematic #1):**
```
a lone astronaut standing on the surface of Mars, dramatic golden hour lighting casting long shadows, dust particles suspended in the atmosphere, anamorphic lens flare, shot on 35mm film, cinematic --ar 21:9 --s 500 --v 6
```

**What changed:** Specific composition, named lighting, added atmosphere, specified film stock, widescreen aspect ratio, stylize value set for cinematic mood.

## The Pattern

Every "Before" prompt is:
- One noun + one style word
- No lighting
- No palette
- No camera
- No aspect ratio
- No imperfections

Every "After" prompt is:
- Specific subject with details
- Named style reference
- Explicit lighting
- Specific color palette
- Camera/composition specified
- Aspect ratio set
- Imperfections or texture cues for realism

**The gap between "AI art newbie" and "AI art pro" is just these details.** This pack bakes them in. You just copy.
