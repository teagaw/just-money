# 10 Styles — Quick Index

Find your style. Copy the prompts. Ship the art.

## 1. Cinematic
**File:** `prompts/01-cinematic.md`
Film stills, movie posters, dramatic lighting. For thumbnails, book covers, ads, anything that needs to look expensive. Pairs with widescreen 21:9 or 2:3 portrait ratios.

## 2. Anime
**File:** `prompts/02-anime.md`
Uses `--niji 6` for authentic anime rendering. Shōnen action, shōjo romance, slice of life, cyberpunk, mecha. Studio Ghibli to modern Trigger vibes.

## 3. Risograph
**File:** `prompts/03-risograph.md`
Grainy 2-color limited palette prints. Indie zine, band poster, art school aesthetic. The duotone registration-misalignment look is the whole vibe.

## 4. Vaporwave
**File:** `prompts/04-vaporwave.md`
80s/90s nostalgia. Neon pink, cyan, purple. Greek statues, palm trees, chrome, grid floors, glitchy text. Aesthetic dream.

## 5. 3D Render
**File:** `prompts/05-3d-render.md`
Octane/Blender/Cinema 4D look. Glossy, soft shadows, abstract shapes, product mockups, character renders. Looks like a Behance shot.

## 6. Watercolor
**File:** `prompts/06-watercolor.md`
Soft washes, paper texture, ink line work, wet-on-wet bleeding. Botanical illustration, landscape, editorial. Calm and classy.

## 7. Oil Painting
**File:** `prompts/07-oil-painting.md`
Thick impasto, classical composition, Dutch masters, Renaissance chiaroscuro. For when you want it to look like it belongs in a museum.

## 8. Isometric
**File:** `prompts/08-isometric.md`
30° game-engine angle. Clean lines, no perspective distortion. Cozy rooms, city blocks, RPG levels, infographics. Perfect for game devs and tech illustrations.

## 9. Brutalist
**File:** `prompts/09-brutalist.md`
Raw concrete, monolithic forms, harsh shadows, imposing scale. Architecture, museums, civic buildings. The "build to last 200 years" aesthetic.

## 10. Soft Pastel
**File:** `prompts/10-soft-pastel.md`
Dreamy pinks, baby blues, lavender, cream. Ballerinas, spring gardens, fluffy clouds, macarons. The cottage-core meets Studio Ghibli sky.

---

## Style Combinations That Slap

- **Cinematic + Soft Pastel** = whimsical movie still (think Amelie)
- **Risograph + Anime** = indie manga zine
- **3D Render + Vaporwave** = Y2K product ad
- **Isometric + Watercolor** = indie game concept art
- **Brutalist + Oil Painting** = Renaissance tech bro poster

## How to Pick

- **Need it for an ad or thumbnail?** → Cinematic, 3D Render, Vaporwave
- **Need it for a print (poster, shirt, mug)?** → Risograph, Soft Pastel, Watercolor
- **Need it for a book cover?** → Oil Painting, Cinematic, Anime
- **Need it for game dev / tech?** → Isometric, 3D Render, Brutalist
- **Need it for Instagram?** → Soft Pastel, Vaporwave, Risograph
