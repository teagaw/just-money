# Mockup Prompts

5 prompts to generate product mockups for Etsy listings, Gumroad, and social posts. These show the pack in use.

---

## Mockup 1: Tablet on Desk

```
a sleek modern tablet on a minimalist wooden desk, the tablet screen shows a beautiful Midjourney-generated artwork, warm afternoon light from a window, a small ceramic coffee cup next to the tablet, clean and modern interior, lifestyle product photography, shallow depth of field --ar 16:9 --s 400 --v 6
```

**Use for:** Etsy listing main image, Gumroad header.

---

---

## Mockup Variations

For each mockup, you can re-roll with different aspect ratios for different platforms:
- **Etsy main image:** `--ar 1:1` or `--ar 4:5`
- **Etsy secondary images:** `--ar 1:1` (square, 2000x2000)
- **Gumroad hero:** `--ar 16:9` or `--ar 21:9`
- **Pinterest pin:** `--ar 2:3`
- **Instagram post:** `--ar 1:1` or `--ar 4:5`
- **Instagram story:** `--ar 9:16`

---

## Screenshot Mockups (the alternative)

For mockups that look like actual Midjourney screenshots, you can:

1. Run a real prompt in Midjourney
2. Screenshot the Discord window showing the 4-image grid
3. Add a subtle border/frame in Canva
4. Use as a "this is what the output looks like" mockup

The screenshot route is more authentic. The AI-generated mockup route is more flexible.

---

## Recommended Mockup Set for Listing

For maximum conversion, use these 5 mockups in this order:

1. **Mockup 1** (tablet on desk) — primary listing image
2. **Mockup 3** (gallery wall) — shows scale and variety
3. **Mockup 4** (notebook flatlay) — for the dark/moody aesthetic
4. **Mockup 2** (phone) — mobile-first
5. **Mockup 5** (multi-device) — premium positioning

Each one reinforces: this is for serious creators, the outputs are real, and you'll be inspired to use them.
