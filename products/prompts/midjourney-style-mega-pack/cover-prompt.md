# Cover Image Prompt

The cover image for this pack: a 3x3 grid showing a single subject rendered in all 10 styles. Visually demonstrates the range.

---

## Cover Prompt v1 (3x3 grid, 10 styles)

```
a single red rose on a black pedestal, rendered simultaneously in 10 distinct artistic styles: cinematic film still, anime cel-shaded, risograph two-color print, vaporwave neon, glossy 3D render, soft watercolor, classical oil painting, isometric low-poly, brutalist concrete, and soft pastel dreamy, 3x3 grid showing each style in its own panel, clean white borders between panels, professional portfolio layout --ar 1:1 --c 15 --s 600 --v 6
```

**Parameters:**
- `--ar 1:1` (square for Etsy, Gumroad, social)
- `--c 15` (a little chaos for variety across panels)
- `--s 600` (medium-high stylization for visual interest)

**Expected output:** A 3x3 grid where each cell shows the same red rose in a different style. Note: Midjourney may not hit exactly 9 distinct styles — you may need to re-roll or use inpainting. If only 4 styles come through, use it as the top-left and crop.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Generate v1 or v2 in Midjourney
2. Pick the best 4-grid or 9-grid result
3. Upscale the best version
4. Open in Photoshop/Canva
5. Add the title text "Midjourney Style Mega Pack" in a clean, modern serif (Playfair Display, EB Garamond, or Cormorant) or sans-serif (Inter, Söhne)
6. Add subtitle: "250 Hand-Tested Prompts • 10 Styles"
7. Add your brand mark
8. Export at 2400x2400 for Etsy (recommended: 2000x2000 minimum, higher is better)
