# Instructions — How to Edit the Bundle in Canva

This guide is for the buyer. It walks through every common edit she will need to make to the templates, in plain language, with the exact button-clicks.

If the buyer reads nothing else, she should read **Section 1 (Opening the template)**, **Section 2 (Swapping her brand colors in 30 seconds)**, and **Section 6 (Exporting at the right size)**. Those three cover 80% of what she'll do.

The other sections are for the moment she wants to do something more specific: change a font, replace a photo, resize for stories, or set up a Canva Brand Kit so she never has to recolor a template by hand again.

## 1. Opening the template

When you receive the bundle, you'll get a link (or a downloaded `.canva` file) for each design file. The 50 square post templates are in one file. The 10 story templates are in a second file. The brand guidelines 1-pager and setup guide are PDFs.

**If you got a link:**

1. Click the link. It opens Canva and creates a copy in your account.
2. You're now on the editor. The first page is the cover. Use the arrows at the bottom of the canvas (or the page list on the left) to navigate between the 50 designs.
3. Each design is on its own page. Click any page to open it.

**If you got a downloaded file:**

1. Open Canva. Click your profile icon (top-right) → **Settings** → **Import** (or use the dedicated import link if Canva prompts you).
2. Select the file. Each page becomes an editable layer.

**First thing to do:** Rename the design. Top-left corner, click the title, type something like "[Your Brand] Instagram Templates" so you can find it again.

**Pro move:** Star the design (top-right ★). It will appear at the top of your "Recent designs" for fast access.

## 2. Swapping the brand colors in 30 seconds

The templates ship with the warm editorial palette (ink, cream, terracotta, sage, bone). Most buyers will want to swap in their own brand colors. Here's the fastest way:

### Option A — Canva Pro "Styles" palette swap (the 30-second option)

1. With the design open, click the **"Design"** button in the top-left of the editor.
2. Look for the **"Styles"** tab (in the left-hand panel).
3. Click the **"Colors"** palette icon. You'll see a few preset palettes.
4. Click **"Add a new palette"** (the + icon).
5. Type in your five colors. Use the same hex codes your brand uses. Recommended token names: `primary`, `secondary`, `accent`, `light`, `dark`.
6. Save the palette.
7. Click your new palette — every color in the design updates instantly.

**Important:** The "Styles" feature is part of **Canva Pro**. If you're on the free plan, use Option B below.

### Option B — Manual color swap (works on free plan)

1. Open the design and pick any element (a text box, a shape, a background rectangle).
2. Click the **color swatch** at the top of the editor (above the canvas, just under the page navigation).
3. Click the **"+"** at the top of the color picker.
4. Type your hex code. Press Enter.
5. The element updates.
6. **To swap the same color across the whole design:** After picking your color, look at the top of the color picker for a small chain-link icon or "Apply to all" button. If available, click it. If not, you'll have to update each element (the bundle is set up so the same color appears in only a few places per design, so it's usually under 10 clicks per template).

**Time estimate:** Manual swap = 2-3 minutes per template for a 5-color brand. Multiply by 50 templates = 100-150 minutes for the full set. Or batch it: edit all 50 in one sitting, swapping one color at a time across all of them. You'll finish in about 45 minutes.

**Pro move:** Add your brand colors to Canva's color picker (the "Document colors" section of the color picker). They stay available for every future design.

### The 5-color rule

The design system uses exactly 5 colors. If your brand has more than 5, pick the most-used 5. If your brand has fewer, the design will still work — extra slots just won't be used.

**Mapping guide** (replace the bundle palette with your brand):

| Bundle token | What to replace it with | Why |
|--------------|------------------------|-----|
| `ink` | Your darkest brand color (near-black for contrast) | The main type color |
| `cream` | Your lightest brand color (light enough for AA contrast) | The main background |
| `terracotta` | Your primary accent | CTAs, badges, emphasis |
| `sage` | Your secondary accent | Hairlines, secondary moments |
| `bone` | A near-white version of your `cream` | Secondary backgrounds |

## 3. Changing the fonts

The templates ship with **Fraunces** (display) and **Inter** (body). Both are free in Canva. To change them:

1. Click any text element to select it.
2. In the top toolbar, click the **font name** (e.g., "Fraunces").
3. A font dropdown opens. Type the name of your preferred font, or browse the "Suggested" list.
4. Click your font. The selected text updates.

**To change all instances of a font across the design:**

1. Press **Ctrl + A** (Windows) or **Cmd + A** (Mac) to select everything on the page.
2. Then hold **Shift** and click the **font name** dropdown.
3. You'll see an "Apply to all" option. Click it.

**Font pairing that works:**

- **Display (Fraunces slot):** Playfair Display, Lora, DM Serif Display, Cormorant, Libre Caslon, Source Serif, Libre Baskerville
- **Body (Inter slot):** Inter (keep it), Montserrat, Poppins, Work Sans, DM Sans, Lato, Source Sans

**Don't mix:** Script fonts in display + Inter in body (the look is exhausting). Two serifs in the same post (the eye doesn't know which is the display). A display font under 80pt (display fonts are designed to be big).

**Forbidden:** Comic Sans, Papyrus, anything in Canva's "Fun" category for a small business brand.

## 4. Replacing a photo

Some templates have a photo placeholder (circular portrait, square product shot, full-bleed background). The placeholder is a gray rectangle with a small photo icon. To replace it:

1. **Click the placeholder** once to select it.
2. Either:
   - **Drag a photo** from your computer directly onto the placeholder, OR
   - Click the placeholder's **"Replace"** button (top-right) → choose "Image" → upload or pick from your library, OR
   - **Right-click** the placeholder → "Replace" → choose source.
3. The placeholder swaps to your photo. The frame, mask, and position stay the same.

**Photo tips for the bundle's warm editorial look:**

- **Warm tone filter.** In the photo editor (double-click the image), click **"Edit image"** → **"Adjust"**. Bump **Warmth** to +20 to +40. Drop **Saturation** to -10 if needed. This integrates photos with the cream/terracotta palette.
- **Square crop.** Click the image, then **"Edit image"** → **"Crop"**. The template is built around a 1:1 square. The placeholder usually already enforces this.
- **Portrait crops** (for B9, B7, P10, B2, B5): Use Canva's built-in 4:5 or 1:1 crop. Hands-at-work, flat-lay, founder portraits work best. No corporate stock photos. No people with cheesy grins.
- **Background removal** (Canva Pro): Click the photo → **"Edit image"** → **"BG Remover"**. Use sparingly — clean cutouts are great; sloppy ones are obvious.

**Where to find good photos if you don't have any yet:**

- Your own phone camera (warm light, candid, hands-at-work)
- Canva's free photo library (search "flat lay," "hands at work," "founder portrait")
- Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay (free, no attribution required)
- Etsy Creative Market (paid, higher quality)

**Stock photo styles to avoid:**

- Office handshakes
- People in suits at laptops
- "Diverse office team" stock photos
- Smiling-with-thumbs-up energy
- People staring at cameras with perfect hair

These will fight the editorial direction of the bundle.

## 5. Adding your logo / signature

Every template has a bottom signature zone — a 12pt Inter UPPERCASE slot for `@yourhandle` or a small logo.

**To add text signature:**

1. Click the existing placeholder text (it says "@yourhandle" in the templates).
2. Type your actual Instagram handle, business name, or website.
3. Resize if needed by selecting and dragging the corner handles (or use the font size selector in the top toolbar).

**To add a logo:**

1. **Delete** the placeholder text box (click it, press Delete).
2. From the left panel, click **"Uploads"** → upload your logo (PNG with transparent background is best).
3. Drag your logo onto the canvas where the placeholder was.
4. Resize by dragging the corner handles while holding **Shift** (to keep proportions).
5. Position it inside the 80px signature zone at the bottom of the canvas (between y=1000 and y=1080).

**Logo tips:**

- Keep your logo under 200px wide in this zone — bigger and it competes with the design.
- A monochrome logo (single color) works best. Multi-color logos get visually noisy at this size.
- If your logo is text-based (like a wordmark), it can replace the @handle entirely. Just make sure it's legible at the size you choose.

**Once for the whole bundle:**

After you set your signature on the first design, copy it: select the signature element, **Ctrl+C**, navigate to each other design, **Ctrl+V**. Then position. Saves you typing your handle 50 times.

## 6. Exporting at the right size (PNG / JPG / PDF)

When your design is done, exporting is the final step.

**For Instagram (most common):**

1. Top-right, click the **"Share"** button.
2. Click **"Download"**.
3. **File type:**
   - **PNG** for Instagram posts and stories (transparent background option, larger file). Best quality.
   - **JPG** for smaller file sizes, faster uploads. Slight quality loss (usually invisible).
   - **PDF** for archival or printing (Canva has a "PDF Print" option for high-quality print).
4. **Size:** Pick the largest size available. The templates are 1080 × 1080 (square) or 1080 × 1920 (story). The largest export will be 2-4× the source size for retina-quality on phones.
5. Click **"Download"**.

**File naming (so you can find them later):**

Before clicking download, click the file name field and rename it:
- `Jane-Smith_Quote-Post_Q1_Final-1.png`
- `Jane-Smith_Sale-Promo_P2_Tuesday.png`

**Schedule a post directly (Canva Pro):**

1. Click **"Share"** → **"More"** → **"Schedule"**.
2. Connect your Instagram account (one-time, in Settings).
3. Pick a date and time.
4. Add your caption (paste from `caption-templates.md`).
5. Click **"Schedule"**. Canva will auto-publish it.

## 7. Resizing a square post to a story (1080 × 1920)

The 50 templates are square (1080 × 1080). Instagram Stories are vertical (1080 × 1920). Sometimes you want a story version of a square post.

**Method 1 — Canva's "Resize" tool (Canva Pro):**

1. With the design open, click **"Resize"** in the top toolbar.
2. Click **"Create new design"**.
3. Set dimensions to **1080 × 1920**.
4. Click **"Resize"**.
5. Canva creates a copy at the new size. Most of the design will scale and recenter. You may need to:
   - Increase the font size on the hero type (Stories are taller, so the type can be bigger)
   - Reposition the hero type to the vertical center (not the square's center)
   - Resize any photo to fill the new dimensions

**Method 2 — Use a Story template instead (recommended):**

The bundle ships with 10 story templates that are pre-sized for 1080 × 1920. For most posts, just use the matching story template. Save yourself the resize.

**The 10 stories in the bundle:**
- S1: Quote
- S2: Engagement poll
- S3: Product reveal
- S4: Testimonial
- S5: Daily highlight cover
- S6: Question box
- S7: Behind-the-scenes
- S8: Swipe-up promo
- S9: "New post" alert
- S10: Countdown

These mirror the 5 categories, so there's a story template for every post type you'll make.

**Safe zones on Stories (important!):**

Instagram UI overlays the top and bottom of every story:
- **Top 250 px:** Your profile picture, username, and the X close button sit here. Don't put important content in this strip.
- **Bottom 250 px:** The "Send Message" input and emoji reactions sit here. Don't put your CTA here either.

The bundle's story templates are designed with these safe zones in mind. When you customize, respect the safe zones.

## 8. Setting up the Canva Brand Kit (one-time, 5 minutes)

The Brand Kit is the single most important setup step. It saves you from recoloring templates ever again. **Canva Pro only.**

**What is the Brand Kit?**

It's where you save your brand's colors, fonts, and logos. Once set up, every new design can use them as defaults. You can also apply them to existing designs in one click.

**How to set it up:**

1. Open Canva. Click your profile icon (top-right).
2. Click **"Brand Kit"** (in the left menu, or under "Brand" in the side panel).
3. **Add colors:** Click "Add color," type your hex code, name it (e.g., "Primary," "Accent"). Repeat for all your brand colors. Recommended: at least 3 colors, max 5.
4. **Add fonts:** Click "Add font," search for your display font, click it. Add your body font the same way.
5. **Add logos:** Click "Upload logo," drag your logo files in. Upload at least one version (ideally also a "light" version for dark backgrounds).
6. **Click "Done."** The Brand Kit is saved.

**How to use it on a design:**

1. Open any template.
2. Click any element. The color picker will show your Brand Kit colors at the top.
3. Pick the right color. It applies.
4. To apply the brand fonts: select the text, change to your display or body font.

**One-time Brand Kit = 5 minutes. Saves you hours across 50 templates.**

## 9. Saving your finished design

**Save (auto-save):**

Canva auto-saves every change. You don't need to do anything.

**Manual save / make a copy:**

If you want a frozen "before I edited" version, click **"File"** (or the kebab menu ⋮) → **"Make a copy"**. This duplicates the design. Now you have one "clean master" and one "your edit."

**Folder organization:**

Create a folder: "Instagram Small Biz Bundle" in your Canva home screen. Drag your design into it. Within the folder, you can have sub-folders:
- 50 Square Templates (clean master)
- 50 Square Templates (your edits)
- 10 Story Templates
- Brand Guidelines 1-Pager
- Setup Guide

**Sharing with a virtual assistant (VA):**

If you hire a VA to help you post, click **"Share"** → **"Invite"** → enter their email. Set their permission to **"Can edit"**. They can edit, post, and schedule but cannot delete the design.

## 10. Pro tips (7 of them)

These are the moves that separate "I made a Canva post" from "I made a Canva post that *looks expensive.*"

### Tip 1 — Batch-create on Sundays

Don't make one post a day. Sit down for 2-3 hours on a Sunday, make 7-10 posts in one sitting, schedule them for the week. The aesthetic decisions are fresh in your mind. The momentum compounds. You'll never stare at a blank Canva again on a Tuesday morning.

### Tip 2 — Use the same 3 designs on repeat, then rotate

Most buyers use 2-3 designs they love and ignore the rest. That's fine — but every 6-8 weeks, force yourself to use 2-3 designs you've never used. The visual variety in your feed is what makes it look like a designer runs it. Treat the unused designs as a rotation pool, not dead weight.

### Tip 3 — Re-shoot your photo library once a quarter

The bundle looks best with warm-toned, candid, hands-at-work photos. Spend 2 hours every 3 months shooting:
- 20 product photos (varied angles, different surfaces)
- 10 hands-at-work shots (you making, packing, holding the thing)
- 5 founder portraits (candid, not posed)
- 5 workspace shots (desk, studio, kitchen counter)

This becomes your photo library. Rotate from it across the quarter. By month 3 you'll have shot 80+ photos. The bundle will never look the same twice.

### Tip 4 — One Fraunces moment per post, always

The bundle's typographic rule is **one display moment per post**. If you find yourself adding a second big Fraunces headline to a post, delete one. The single big type is what makes the design feel like a magazine cover. Two big types make it feel like a PowerPoint.

### Tip 5 — Always export at 2× the canvas size

When you download, pick the largest size available. The templates are 1080 × 1080. The "Largest" export will give you 2160 × 2160 (or 4× for retina). Instagram's actual upload size cap is 1080 wide, but the larger file uploads at higher quality and looks sharper on retina displays. Cost: a few extra seconds. Benefit: noticeably crisper posts.

### Tip 6 — Recolor the whole bundle in one session

Don't recolor one post at a time. Set up your Brand Kit (Section 8) once, then open each design and apply the Brand Kit. The whole set recolors in about 30-45 minutes for all 50 designs. Do this in one sitting, and you only have to do it once.

### Tip 7 — Save your "house style" version as a template

After you finish editing a design, click **"..."** (kebab menu) → **"Save as template"**. This saves the design as a reusable template inside your Canva account. The next time you need a similar post (quote, testimonial, product promo), you don't start from scratch — you start from your own finished version, with your colors, fonts, and signature already in place. Over time, you'll build a library of 50 personal templates that are uniquely yours.

## Troubleshooting

**"The colors don't look right after I added them to the Brand Kit."**

- The Brand Kit shows the most-recently-added colors first. Scroll down to find older ones.
- The "Document colors" section of the color picker has only the colors used in the current design. The "Brand Kit" section has all your saved colors.

**"The fonts I chose look different than expected."**

- Canva's font rendering varies slightly across browsers. If the type looks off, check the actual size (top toolbar) and weight (Bold / Light / Regular dropdown).
- Avoid weights Canva doesn't support. Fraunces, for example, only ships with Regular, Medium, and Bold. Trying to set weight 700 = it uses Bold. Trying weight 500 = it uses Medium.

**"My photo looks too dark in the design."**

- Click the photo → **"Edit image"** → **"Adjust"** → bump **Brightness** to +10 to +20 and **Contrast** to -10 to +5. Warm-toned photos tend to need a brightness boost in the cream-toned bundle.

**"The text wraps differently than the example."**

- Expected. Your copy will be longer or shorter than the example. Adjust the text box size or font size until it fits. The 4pt grid is forgiving: font sizes that snap to multiples of 4 (24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 44, 48, 56, 64, 72, 88, 96) feel designed. Other sizes (e.g., 35, 47) feel off.

**"I made a change and want to undo."**

- **Ctrl + Z** (Windows) or **Cmd + Z** (Mac). Canva supports multi-step undo.
- For a more drastic recovery, Canva keeps version history. Click **"File"** → **"Version history"** (sometimes under the kebab menu). You can roll back to any previous save.

**"I need help I can't find in this guide."**

- Canva's official help center: help.canva.com
- Canva Design School (free courses): canva.com/designschool
- The bundle's specific design choices are documented in `design-system.md`. If you're wondering "why is this color `terracotta` and not `sage`?", the answer is in the design system file.

## Quick-Reference Checklist (use before publishing)

Before you hit publish on any post, run this 6-point checklist:

- [ ] **Colors** match your brand kit (or the bundle's default palette, if you haven't customized)
- [ ] **Fonts** are Fraunces (display) + Inter (body) — or your chosen pair
- [ ] **Copy** fits the template — no overflow, no awkwardly short lines
- [ ] **Photo** (if any) is warm-toned, high-quality, well-cropped
- [ ] **Signature** is your actual handle, not `@yourhandle`
- [ ] **Safe zones** are respected — nothing important in the bottom 80px of a square post, nothing in the top/bottom 250px of a story

If all six are checked, publish. You are done.

That's it. You have everything you need. The hardest part is the first post. After that, it's a system.
