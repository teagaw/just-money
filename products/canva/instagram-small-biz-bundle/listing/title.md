# Etsy Title — 3 A/B Variants

Etsy titles have a **140-character limit** (technically 140 chars including spaces, but the practical limit is 140). Every title must:

- Lead with the **primary keyword** ("Instagram Templates")
- Include **secondary keywords** the buyer is searching for
- Stay **scannable** — Etsy buyers skim, not read
- Not stuff keywords — Etsy penalizes keyword-stuffed titles
- Read naturally enough that the buyer knows what she's getting

The format that works: **[primary keyword] [secondary keyword] [tertiary keyword] | [what's inside] | [audience or outcome]**

The pipe `|` character breaks the title into scannable sections without taking up too much space. Commas and dashes also work, but pipes create a clearer visual rhythm in the search results page.

The 3 variants below are designed for A/B testing. Run each for 30 days. Compare conversion rate. Keep the winner. The variants test different angles:

- **Variant A** — lead with the volume (50+ templates)
- **Variant B** — lead with the audience (small business)
- **Variant C** — lead with the outcome (scroll-stopping feed)

All three are designed to rank for the core search terms. None are keyword-stuffed. All three fit within 140 characters.

---

## Variant A — "Volume-Led"

**Angle:** "50+ templates" is the primary hook. Leads with the number, follows with the format and the audience.

```
Instagram Templates Canva | 50+ Editable Post Designs for Small Business Owners | Quotes, Testimonials, Tips, Product Promo, BTS
```

**Character count:** 137
**Primary keyword:** "Instagram Templates Canva"
**Secondary keywords:** "Editable Post Designs," "Small Business Owners," "Quotes," "Testimonials," "Tips," "Product Promo," "BTS"
**Tertiary keywords (long-tail):** "50+ Editable Post Designs," "Instagram Templates for Small Business"
**Best for:** Buyers who search by volume / want to see "more bang for the buck"
**Estimated click-through:** Highest for buyers who are *price-comparing* multiple bundles

### Why this works

- "50+" in the first 15 characters is a number that catches the eye
- "Editable" tells the buyer she'll be able to customize — not just download
- The category list (Quotes, Testimonials, etc.) tells the buyer she'll get a *system*, not 50 versions of the same template
- "Small Business Owners" is the specific audience signal

### What to watch

- If the listing is underperforming, this variant may be too "listy" — buyers who want the *system* (Category 4: Tips, etc.) may not stop on the list
- A/B test against Variant C (outcome-led) before declaring a winner

## Variant B — "Audience-Led"

**Angle:** "Small business" is the primary hook. The buyer identifies as a small business owner first; templates are the *thing she needs*.

```
Instagram Templates for Small Business | 50+ Canva Posts, Stories, Captions & Hashtags | Cohesive Feed in One Click
```

**Character count:** 130
**Primary keyword:** "Instagram Templates for Small Business"
**Secondary keywords:** "Canva Posts," "Stories," "Captions," "Hashtags," "Cohesive Feed"
**Tertiary keywords (long-tail):** "50+ Canva Posts Stories Captions," "Cohesive Feed in One Click"
**Best for:** Buyers who self-identify as small business owners and are looking for a *complete* content system, not just templates
**Estimated click-through:** Highest for the buyer who has bought "just templates" before and was frustrated by the lack of captions/hashtags

### Why this works

- "Instagram Templates for Small Business" is the exact phrase the target buyer types
- The list (Posts, Stories, Captions, Hashtags) signals "complete system" — differentiators 2 and 3 from `README.md`
- "Cohesive Feed in One Click" speaks directly to the buyer's *real* pain (every post looks like a different brand)
- The emotional hook is "cohesion" — buyers want a feed that *looks like a brand*

### What to watch

- The phrase "Small Business" in the title can sometimes trigger Etsy's algorithm to rank the listing lower for buyers searching "Instagram Templates" generically (without the small business qualifier)
- A/B test against Variant A (volume-led) to see which ranks for more *total* search impressions

## Variant C — "Outcome-Led"

**Angle:** "Scroll-stopping feed" is the primary hook. The buyer is shopping for a *result*, not a product. She wants her feed to look better.

```
Scroll-Stopping Instagram Templates | 50+ Canva Posts for Small Business | Editorial Design System, Captions & Calendar
```

**Character count:** 131
**Primary keyword:** "Instagram Templates"
**Secondary keywords:** "Canva Posts," "Small Business," "Editorial Design System," "Captions," "Calendar"
**Tertiary keywords (long-tail):** "Scroll-Stopping Instagram Templates," "Editorial Design System"
**Best for:** Buyers who are shopping for a *result* — a more beautiful, more professional feed — not a list of features
**Estimated click-through:** Highest for buyers who have been burned by "ugly" templates before and are looking for an editorial / premium feel

### Why this works

- "Scroll-Stopping" is the only word in the title that's a *feeling* — it speaks to the buyer's hope for her own feed
- "Editorial Design System" is a unique differentiator. Most competitors say "template bundle." This says "system" — which signals higher quality and a coherent design philosophy
- "Captions & Calendar" reinforces the complete-system angle
- The "for Small Business" qualifier still anchors the audience

### What to watch

- "Scroll-Stopping" is a more emotional / less search-volume phrase. If Etsy search volume is the bottleneck, this variant may get fewer impressions
- If impressions are the bottleneck, A/B test against Variant B (audience-led), which has the highest search-volume keywords

## A/B Testing Protocol

Run all three variants for **30 days each**. Track these metrics in Etsy's Shop Manager → Stats → Listing Stats:

| Metric | What it tells you |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Impressions** | How many buyers saw the title in search results |
| **Clicks** | How many buyers clicked through to the listing |
| **Click-through rate (CTR)** | Clicks ÷ Impressions — measures title effectiveness |
| **Conversion rate** | Orders ÷ Visits — measures listing-page effectiveness |
| **Orders** | Total sales attributed to that title variant |
| **Revenue** | Total $ attributed to that title variant |

**Decision rule:**

- If Variant A has the highest **orders**, keep it (volume wins for some audiences)
- If Variant B has the highest **orders**, keep it (audience-specific keywords convert for niche buyers)
- If Variant C has the highest **orders**, keep it (outcome-led titles convert when the buyer's pain is visual)

**If all three perform similarly** (within 10% of each other), pick the one you like best. A/B tests are a directional signal, not a science.

**Run one variant at a time.** Don't run all three in parallel and compare — Etsy search ranking fluctuates enough that you can't isolate the variable. Rotate sequentially: A for 30 days, B for 30 days, C for 30 days.

## Title Construction Notes

### What works

- **Front-load the primary keyword.** The first 3-5 words carry the most weight in Etsy's search algorithm.
- **Use pipes `|` to break up sections.** Pipes are scannable and don't waste characters.
- **Include 2-3 long-tail phrases.** "50+ Canva Posts for Small Business" is a long-tail; "Scroll-Stopping Instagram Templates" is another.
- **Mention what's in the bundle.** "Captions" and "Calendar" tell the buyer she's not just getting pictures.

### What doesn't work

- **ALL CAPS SECTIONS.** Etsy demotes ALL CAPS titles in search. Use sentence case.
- **Special characters.** ★, ♥, →, ✦ — Etsy strips these from search indexing. They also look spammy.
- **Keyword stuffing.** "Instagram Instagram Instagram Templates Templates Posts Posts" — Etsy penalizes this. Read your title out loud. If it sounds like a robot, rewrite.
- **Unrealistic claims.** "BEST TEMPLATES EVER!!!", "GO VIRAL" — Etsy demotes these.
- **Emoji.** Some sellers use them. Etsy doesn't formally penalize them, but they reduce professionalism. Skip.
- **Numbers in words vs. digits.** "50+" works better than "Fifty Plus." Etsy indexes digits.

### The 140-character reality

Every character matters. Test your title in a character counter before publishing. Common mistakes that push titles over:

- "Canva" spelled out instead of "Canva" (no difference, just be aware)
- "Instagram" instead of "IG" (always use the full word; Etsy indexes it better)
- Apostrophes and quotes (each one is a character)
- Multiple spaces in a row (Etsy strips these, but it's wasted thinking time)

### Beyond 140: The "Tags" complement

The title is 140 characters of search signal. The **13 Etsy tags** (in `tags.md`) are the *other* 260 characters (13 × 20). The title and the tags work together:

- **Title** = what the buyer sees in search results
- **Tags** = what the search algorithm uses to surface the listing

Don't repeat the same keywords in both. The title and the tags should be **complementary**, not redundant. If "Instagram Templates" is in the title, the tags should focus on what's *not* in the title: "50+ editable," "social media kit," "quote post templates," "cohesive feed," "small biz marketing," etc.

See `tags.md` for the full tag list.
