# Etsy Tags & Long-Tail Keywords

## The 13 Etsy Tags

Etsy allows exactly **13 tags** per listing. Each tag is a multi-word phrase, **20 characters max** (including spaces). Tags are how Etsy's search algorithm decides which search queries your listing surfaces for. They work in tandem with the title — the title is what the buyer *sees*, the tags are what the algorithm *uses*.

**The rules:**

- 13 tags, exactly. No more, no fewer.
- 20 characters per tag, including spaces.
- Multi-word phrases outperform single words. "Instagram templates" (20 chars) beats "Instagram" (9 chars) for the same search volume.
- Don't repeat title keywords verbatim. The title and tags are complementary, not redundant.
- No commas. No special characters. Just words and spaces.
- Don't include the brand name, the word "etsy," or the word "shop."

The 13 tags below cover the **search intents** a small business owner has when she's looking for this bundle. Each tag targets a different way she might search.

### Quick check on tag 13

The "etsy" suffix is generally a poor tag choice (Etsy deprioritizes it), but buyers do search "template bundle etsy" to find template bundles specifically on Etsy. The 20-character limit forces a compromise. The tag `template bundle ets` is the practical workaround. Some sellers omit this tag and replace with `bundle pack` (11 chars) or `canva bundle` (12 chars) — both are valid alternatives. The final decision depends on the A/B test.

## 20 Long-Tail Keywords

The 20 long-tail keywords below are **not used as Etsy tags** (which are limited to 13, 20-char phrases). They are used in:

- The **listing description** (sprinkled naturally — never stuffed)
- The **listing photos** (text overlay on the cover and the mockups)
- **Pinterest pin descriptions**
- **Etsy "Categories" and "Attributes"** fields (separate from tags)
- **Social media posts** promoting the listing
- **The shop's "About" section**
- **The shop's "Announcements"** (the rotating banner at the top of the shop)
- **Email marketing** to the buyer's list

These are the phrases real people type when they're searching for what this bundle solves. They are slightly longer than the 13 tags (because they don't have the 20-char limit) and more specific (because they're not competing for the same broad terms as the title).

### How to use these

**In the description:** Pick 5-7 of the most relevant long-tails. Weave them in naturally. They should not feel stuffed; the description should read like normal product copy. The relevant long-tails for the existing description are:

- `instagram templates for small business` (used in the "Who This Is For" section)
- `instagram content calendar template` (used in the "What's Inside" section)
- `instagram caption templates` (used in the "What's Inside" section)
- `instagram hashtag sets for small business` (covered in the hashtag sets section)
- `cohesive instagram feed template` (used in the "Differentiation" section)
- `scroll stopping instagram templates` (used in the title and "Differentiation")
- `instagram bundle for entrepreneurs` (the headline value)

**In the photos:** The cover image should contain the phrase "50+ Templates" or "Instagram Bundle." The mockups can include "Editable in Canva" or "Small Business Ready" in their corners. Photo text helps both Pinterest SEO and Etsy search.

**In the Categories / Attributes fields:** Etsy's category structure has specific attributes. Set the primary category to "Crafts & Tools" → "Paper & Party Supplies" → "Paper Crafts" → "Stencils & Templates" (or whatever the closest fit is). The attributes for that category may include "Type," "Style," "Occasion," etc. Use those fields — they index for Etsy search too.

## Tag Maintenance Schedule

Etsy tag performance drifts over time. Refresh quarterly:

- **Every 90 days,** look at Etsy Shop Manager → Stats → Search Terms. See which search terms are sending traffic to your listing.
- **Compare to your current 13 tags.** If a search term is sending traffic that *isn't* in your current tags, add it (and drop the lowest-performing current tag).
- **Run this refresh four times a year.** The work takes 30 minutes. The upside is 5-15% more impressions, which compounds over time.

### Tag Refresh Protocol

1. Open Etsy Shop Manager → Stats → Search Terms.
2. Sort by "Visits" (default) or filter to "Last 90 days."
3. Identify the top 5-10 search terms bringing traffic.
4. Compare to your current 13 tags.
5. If a top-performing term is missing from your tags, swap in the lowest-performing tag for it.
6. Document the change in a spreadsheet (so you can see what's working over time).

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using the brand name as a tag.** Etsy suppresses this.
- **Using competitor names as tags.** Etsy deprioritizes listings that tag other brands.
- **Using single-word tags.** "Templates" alone is too broad and competes with every template listing. Multi-word phrases outperform.
- **Reusing the same 13 tags across all listings.** Each listing should have unique tags targeted to its specific search intent. If you have 5 listings, all 65 tags should be different.
- **Stuffing high-volume but irrelevant tags.** "Christmas" and "wedding" will get you impressions but no conversions. Etsy tracks conversion rate, and irrelevant tags tank it.
- **Using tags >20 characters.** Etsy silently truncates. You'll lose the words after character 20.

## Tag Performance Indicators

In Etsy Shop Manager → Stats → Listing Stats → Tags (when available), Etsy shows:

- **Impressions** per tag
- **Clicks** per tag
- **Click-through rate** per tag
- **Orders** attributed to each tag

This is the per-tag performance breakdown. Check it every 90 days. The tags sending the most clicks at the highest conversion rate are the ones to keep and double down on. The tags sending impressions but no clicks are the ones to swap.

(If Etsy doesn't show per-tag stats in your account, use the Search Terms report as a proxy. It's the same data, just from the buyer's query side instead of the listing's tag side.)
