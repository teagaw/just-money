# Post Categories — 50 Design Specs

This is the heart of the bundle. Every one of the 50 designs below is a **fully specified** layout: background color, accent color, hero type slot (with pixel-level placement and size), image slot (if any), supporting element, and the scroll-stopping reason. The buyer opens the matching Canva page, drops in her photo and copy, and the post is done in 60 seconds.

All measurements reference the **1080 × 1080 square** canvas unless noted. All color values come from `design-system.md` (ink `#1A1A1A`, cream `#E8DDD0`, terracotta `#D4926A`, sage `#6B8E7A`, bone `#F4F1EC`). All type sizes are in pt at output.

The five categories and the job each one does:

| # | Category | Buyer intent | Average saves / shares |
|---|----------|--------------|------------------------|
| 1 | Quote / Mantra | Inspire, anchor brand voice, get saves | High saves, high shares |
| 2 | Testimonial / Social Proof | Build trust, overcome skepticism | High credibility |
| 3 | Product Promo / Offer | Drive clicks, announce launches, sell | High CTR |
| 4 | Tips / Educational (Carousel) | Build authority, get saves and follows | Highest saves |
| 5 | Behind-the-Scenes / Personal | Build connection, humanize the brand | Highest DMs |

A healthy mix posts all five categories in a recurring weekly cycle. The 30-day calendar in `post-ideas-calendar.md` sequences them.

## Category 1 — Quote / Mantra (10 designs)

The "I needed to hear this today" posts. Highest save-rate. Mostly type-only.

### Q1 — The Centerpiece Quote

The most classic layout. One sentence, one moment, one period.

- **Background:** `cream` (`#E8DDD0`)
- **Accent:** `terracotta` (single use, see below)
- **Type stack:**
  - Hero quote — Fraunces 88 pt, weight 500, tracking -2%, leading 0.95, color `ink`, centered horizontally, vertically positioned 380–680 px from the top
  - Attribution — Inter 14 pt, weight 600, UPPERCASE, tracking +8%, color `ink`, centered, 760 px from top
- **Accent element:** a single 6 px terracotta period at the end of the quote — the only spot of accent color on the post
- **Signature zone:** 12 pt Inter UPPERCASE `@yourhandle` at 1020 px from top, centered
- **Image slot:** none
- **Scroll-stopper:** scale. The quote is so large it almost touches the safe-zone edges. The terracotta period is a tiny surprise.
- **Sample headline:** *"You don't have to be loud to be heard."*

### Q2 — Top-Anchored Pull Quote

For attributed wisdom — a founder's words, a customer epiphany, a mentor line.

- **Background:** `bone` (`#F4F1EC`)
- **Accent:** `sage` (hairline rule, see below)
- **Type stack:**
  - Open-quote mark — Fraunces 240 pt, weight 500, color `sage`, opacity 30%, anchored top-left at (120, 80)
  - Pull quote — Fraunces 48 pt, weight 500, leading 1.1, color `ink`, left-aligned, 200–680 px from top, max width 720 px
  - Attribution — Inter 18 pt, weight 400, italic OFF, color `ink` 70% opacity, left-aligned, 740 px from top
- **Accent element:** a 1 px sage horizontal rule at y=720, 240 px wide, left-anchored
- **Signature zone:** standard, bottom
- **Image slot:** none
- **Scroll-stopper:** the giant ghosted open-quote mark — reads as magazine cover, not Canva template
- **Sample headline:** *"Stop selling. Start serving." — and watch what happens to your revenue.*

### Q3 — Asymmetric Editorial

For when you want the post to feel like a printed editorial spread, not a Pinterest pin.

- **Background:** `cream` (`#E8DDD0`)
- **Accent:** `terracotta`
- **Type stack:**
  - Vertical date stamp — Inter 12 pt, UPPERCASE, tracking +8%, color `terracotta`, rotated -90°, anchored bottom-left at (80, 540)
  - Hero quote — Fraunces 64 pt, weight 500, leading 1.05, color `ink`, right-aligned, 480–880 px from top, max width 600 px, x-offset 360 px
- **Accent element:** a solid terracotta circle, 24 px diameter, at (320, 200) — a deliberate moment of color the eye locks onto
- **Signature zone:** bottom-right instead of bottom-center, 12 pt Inter UPPERCASE `@yourhandle`
- **Image slot:** none
- **Scroll-stopper:** it doesn't look symmetrical. The brain keeps scanning to "fix" it, and that micro-pause is the stop.
- **Sample headline:** *"Build the business you'd want to buy from."*

### Q4 — Stacked Staggered

The rhythm of three lines at three different sizes. Feels like a poem.

- **Background:** `ink` (`#1A1A1A`)
- **Accent:** none (monochrome dark)
- **Type stack:** three Fraunces lines, all cream (`#E8DDD0`), all centered
  - Line 1 — 96 pt, weight 500, y=300
  - Line 2 — 40 pt, weight 400, y=460 (the intentional breath)
  - Line 3 — 64 pt, weight 500, y=600
- **Accent element:** the size contrast itself IS the design
- **Signature zone:** cream Inter UPPERCASE 12 pt at 1020 px, centered
- **Image slot:** none
- **Scroll-stopper:** the rhythm. Reading eye can't help but follow the wave of three lines.
- **Sample headline:** *"Do less. / Notice more. / Charge accordingly."*

### Q5 — Number-Stamped Quote

An editorial designer's trick: a small number in the corner reframes the whole post as a "series."

- **Background:** `bone` (`#F4F1EC`)
- **Accent:** `terracotta`
- **Type stack:**
  - Series number — Fraunces 80 pt, weight 500, color `terracotta`, top-left at (80, 80)
  - Series label — Inter 12 pt, UPPERCASE, tracking +8%, color `ink` 60%, directly under the number, "QUOTE No. 01"
  - Hero quote — Fraunces 52 pt, weight 500, leading 1.1, color `ink`, left-aligned, 280–820 px from top, max width 720 px
- **Accent element:** the terracotta number doubles as accent
- **Signature zone:** standard
- **Image slot:** none
- **Scroll-stopper:** the "No. 01" tells the viewer there's a series — they swipe your profile to find the rest
- **Sample headline:** *"No. 01 — You can have it all. Just not all at once."*

### Q6 — Italic Emphasis

One italic word carries the whole emotional weight.

- **Background:** `cream` (`#E8DDD0`)
- **Accent:** none
- **Type stack:**
  - Surrounding quote — Fraunces 44 pt, weight 500, color `ink`, centered, 380–780 px
  - Emphasis word — Fraunces 120 pt, weight 500, **italic**, color `ink`, replaces the equivalent word in the quote, line wraps as needed
- **Accent element:** the italic word IS the accent
- **Signature zone:** standard
- **Image slot:** none
- **Scroll-stopper:** the size jump from 44pt to 120pt on a single word stops the thumb cold
- **Sample headline:** *"The most *expensive* thing you can do is stay still."*

### Q7 — Quote With Brush Underline

A hand-drawn-feel sage brush stroke under one word. Magazine rather than Instagram.

- **Background:** `cream` (`#E8DDD0`)
- **Accent:** `sage`
- **Type stack:**
  - Hero quote — Fraunces 56 pt, weight 500, leading 1.1, color `ink`, left-aligned, 200–760 px from top, max width 800 px
- **Accent element:** a sage "brush stroke" shape (Canva's organic-blob or a 12 px-tall ellipse stretched to 240 px wide) sitting under one of the words. Use the `sage` color, organic-blob shape with slight transparency (40%).
- **Signature zone:** standard
- **Image slot:** none
- **Scroll-stopper:** the brush stroke is the only "decoration" on the post — and it implies craft, not Canva.
- **Sample headline:** *"Slow growth is still growth — and it doesn't burn out."*

### Q8 — Bordered Frame Quote

A hairline sage border inset 60 px. The quote lives inside, not edge-to-edge.

- **Background:** `cream` (`#E8DDD0`)
- **Accent:** `sage`
- **Type stack:**
  - Hero quote — Fraunces 48 pt, weight 500, leading 1.1, color `ink`, centered horizontally, 320–720 px from top, max width 800 px
  - Attribution — Inter 14 pt, UPPERCASE, tracking +8%, color `ink` 70%, centered, 760 px from top
- **Accent element:** a 1 px sage rectangle, inset 60 px from the canvas edges, forming a 960 × 960 frame
- **Signature zone:** bottom-center, inside the frame
- **Image slot:** none
- **Scroll-stopper:** the frame changes the relationship between text and canvas. Reads as art, not post.
- **Sample headline:** *"If they don't remember the price, they remember the experience."*

### Q9 — Two-Part Quote

A split composition: the *question* on top, the *answer* on the bottom.

- **Background:** `cream` (`#E8DDD0`)
- **Accent:** `terracotta`
- **Type stack:**
  - Top hook line — Fraunces 32 pt, weight 500, color `ink` 60%, italic, centered, 240 px from top ("What if I told you...")
  - Bottom resolution — Fraunces 72 pt, weight 500, color `ink`, centered, 460–820 px ("You can do it scared.")
- **Accent element:** a 1 px terracotta horizontal rule, 200 px wide, centered at y=400
- **Signature zone:** standard
- **Image slot:** none
- **Scroll-stopper:** the two-part structure creates a tiny narrative arc. The reader pauses to read both halves.
- **Sample headline:** *"What if the work isn't to be louder — but to be clearer?"*

### Q10 — The Single Word Manifesto

Maximum impact. One word, no other context.

- **Background:** `bone` (`#F4F1EC`)
- **Accent:** `terracotta` (single period, optional)
- **Type stack:**
  - The word — Fraunces 240 pt, weight 500, color `ink`, centered, 380–680 px from top
  - Optional terracotta period — 12 pt terracotta, directly after the word
  - Attribution — Inter 14 pt, UPPERCASE, tracking +8%, color `ink` 70%, centered, 900 px from top
- **Accent element:** the optional terracotta period — not every post needs it; without it, the design is even more stripped
- **Signature zone:** standard
- **Image slot:** none
- **Scroll-stopper:** scale and emptiness. There's nothing else on the post to look at.
- **Sample headline:** *"Patience."* (or "Momentum." or "Done." — buyer picks one a week, builds a recognizable series.)

## Category 2 — Testimonial / Social Proof (10 designs)

The "I should trust this person" posts. Mix of type, photo, and screenshot frames.

### T1 — Star Rating Hero

Classic 5-star review, elevated.

- **Background:** `cream` (`#E8DDD0`)
- **Accent:** `terracotta` (filled stars)
- **Type stack:**
  - 5 stars — terracotta filled Phosphor thin-line stars, scaled to 48 pt, centered, 280 px from top
  - Review quote — Fraunces 32 pt, weight 500, leading 1.2, color `ink`, centered, 360–680 px from top, max width 800 px
  - Reviewer line — Inter 16 pt, weight 600, UPPERCASE, tracking +8%, color `ink` 70%, centered, 760 px from top (`— JANE D., CUSTOMER`)
  - Date — Inter 12 pt, weight 400, color `ink` 50%, centered, 800 px from top (`May 2026`)
- **Accent element:** the 5 terracotta stars
- **Signature zone:** standard
- **Image slot:** none
- **Scroll-stopper:** the stars above the quote are a visual promise — the brain expects a review and finds it instantly.
- **Sample headline:** *"Best decision I made all year." — Jane D., Verified Buyer*

### T2 — Screenshot Frame

For when the strongest review is a real DM or tweet screenshot. The buyer pastes her screenshot into the phone frame.

- **Background:** `sage` (`#6B8E7A`)
- **Accent:** none
- **Type stack:**
  - Top label — Inter 12 pt, UPPERCASE, tracking +8%, color `bone`, top-left at (80, 80) — `A NOTE FROM A CUSTOMER`
  - Bottom handle label — Inter 14 pt, UPPERCASE, tracking +8%, color `bone`, centered, 1020 px from top — `@yourhandle`
- **Frame element:** a phone-shaped frame, 540 × 1080 px, centered, holding a screenshot image. Use a `bone`-filled rectangle with 48 px corner radius as the phone body; the screenshot goes inside as a 480 × 1020 px rectangle, also 48 px radius. Buyer replaces with a real screenshot.
- **Accent element:** the sage background IS the accent
- **Signature zone:** integrated above (the handle label)
- **Image slot:** the screenshot inside the phone frame — REQUIRED for this template
- **Scroll-stopper:** the recognizable phone shape signals "this is a real review" before the viewer reads a word.
- **Sample headline:** *Real reviews from real people. No editing.*

### T3 — Portrait + Pull Quote

- **Background:** `bone` (`#F4F1EC`)
- **Accent:** `terracotta` (the circle outline)
- **Type stack:**
  - Quote — Fraunces 36 pt, weight 500, leading 1.2, color `ink`, left-aligned at x=540, 320–680 px from top, max width 460 px
  - Quote mark — Fraunces 200 pt, weight 500, color `terracotta`, top-left at (560, 220) — the giant open-quote mark

- **Image element:** circular photo, 380 × 380 px, left side, centered vertically at y=540, x=120. Hairline terracotta ring (2 px) inset 8 px outside the circle.
- **Signature zone:** standard
- **Scroll-stopper:** the terracotta open-quote mark and the photo in their own half — the layout breaks the eye's expectation of centered testimonials.
- **Sample headline:** *"[Name] actually listened to what I wanted — and then built exactly that."*

### T4 — Before / After

For transformation testimonials. Left = before state, right = after outcome.

- **Background:** `cream` (`#E8DDD0`)
- **Accent:** `sage` (arrow + divider)

- **Accent element:** a sage arrow icon (Phosphor thin, 32 pt) centered at (540, 480), pointing right
- **Signature zone:** standard
- **Image slot:** none (text-only)
- **Scroll-stopper:** the arrow implies a journey, not a comparison. The eye moves left to right and pauses.
