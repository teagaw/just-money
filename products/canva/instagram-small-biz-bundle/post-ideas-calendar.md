# 30-Day Posting Calendar — A Full Month of Content

The weekly cycle is the engine. Repeat it four times. Each day has a fixed role, but the topic rotates so the feed never looks identical:

| Day | Post type | Job it does |
|-----|-----------|-------------|
| **Mon** | Tips / Value | Build authority, get saves, get follows |
| **Tue** | Testimonial | Build trust, overcome skepticism |
| **Wed** | Behind-the-Scenes | Humanize the brand, drive DMs |
| **Thu** | Quote / Mantra | Set the week's tone, get shares |
| **Fri** | Product Promo | Drive revenue — soft launch, sale, restock |
| **Sat** | Engagement | Drive comments, surface the post in the algorithm |
| **Sun** | Personal | Build connection, take a breath, humanize |

A 7-day cycle × ~4.3 weeks = 30 days. Below is exactly 30 days, no half-weeks.

For each day you'll find:

- **Type** — which of the 5 categories from `post-categories.md`
- **Topic** — the specific subject for that day
- **Hook** — a suggested first line for the caption (the line that earns the next line)
- **Post design** — the specific numbered template to use (e.g., Q1 = Quote #1, T3 = Testimonial #3, P2 = Product #2, C4 = Carousel #4, B7 = Behind-the-Scenes #7). The mapping is in `post-categories.md`.
- **Caption template** — the matching caption from `caption-templates.md` (e.g., E1, P4, G3)
- **Hashtag set** — which niche set from `hashtag-sets.md` (or "Add 5 brand tags")
- **Why this day** — a one-line strategic reason for the slot

The calendar starts on a **Monday** for clarity. Buyers who start on a different day should rotate the week forward — the order matters, the days don't.

## Week 1 — Establish the brand voice

### Day 1 — Monday · Tips / Value

- **Type:** Tips carousel
- **Topic:** "3 mistakes I made in my first year of [running a small business / selling online / coaching]"
- **Hook:** "I lost $4,200 learning these. You don't have to."
- **Post design:** C1 (Carousel Cover) → C2 (Numbered List Slide) × 3 → C9 (Closing CTA)
- **Caption template:** E2 (Numbered List Drop)
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + the matching niche set
- **Why this day:** Monday is decision-day. People start the week ready to learn. Authority posts land hardest here.

### Day 2 — Tuesday · Testimonial

- **Type:** Single testimonial
- **Topic:** "A note from a customer" — feature one specific, real review
- **Hook:** "Last Tuesday, Sarah DM'd me this:"
- **Post design:** T10 (Star Bar + Single Quote) or T1 (Star Rating Hero) for a polished review; T2 (Screenshot Frame) if the strongest asset is a screenshot
- **Caption template:** P3 (Customer Story Sale)
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + the matching niche set
- **Why this day:** Tuesday is trust-day. The week is moving, people are in buy-mode but not yet researching. Trust-building is the bridge.

### Day 3 — Wednesday · Behind-the-Scenes

- **Type:** Founder / process post
- **Topic:** "What I did this week" — a candid slice of the work
- **Hook:** "Wednesday behind the scenes:"
- **Post design:** B1 (Founder Story Letter), B2 (Studio Peek Photo), or B9 (Process Step Frame) depending on what you shot
- **Caption template:** E10 (Long Read) for a personal story, or P9 (Behind the Product) for a product reveal
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + the matching niche set
- **Why this day:** Mid-week slump. Personal posts break the slide and drive DMs.

### Day 4 — Thursday · Quote / Mantra

- **Type:** Single hero quote
- **Topic:** Pick a value your brand stands for. ("Patience," "Quality over quantity," "Show up even when it's hard.")
- **Hook:** Use the quote itself as the post; the hook is the type
- **Post design:** Q1 (Centerpiece Quote), Q3 (Asymmetric Editorial), Q5 (Number-Stamped), or Q10 (Single Word Manifesto) — pick the one that matches the quote's energy
- **Caption template:** E3 (What I Wish I Knew) or G10 (Quote + Comment Prompt)
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + 5-10 of the small/medium tags from the matching niche
- **Why this day:** Thursday is reflection-day. People are processing the week. Quotes get shared the most on Thursdays.

### Day 5 — Friday · Product Promo

- **Type:** Soft promotion
- **Topic:** "Restock alert" or "What I made this week" — pick whatever is most true to your week
- **Hook:** "It's back. The [product] you [DMed me about / asked for / stalked the site for]."
- **Post design:** P1 (Hero Product Launch), P2 (Sale Badge Hero), P4 (New Arrival Grid), P6 (Restock Notification), P7 (Bundle Deal), or P8 (Price Drop) depending on what you're promoting
- **Caption template:** P1 (Soft Launch), P4 (Restock Alert), or P2 (Sale Announcement)
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + the matching niche set
- **Why this day:** Friday is purchase-day. People are most willing to spend at the end of the work week. Sales posts land hardest here.

### Day 6 — Saturday · Engagement

- **Type:** Question / poll
- **Topic:** "This or that?" / "Which one are you?" / "Pick your fighter."
- **Hook:** A binary question, low friction
- **Post design:** Q1, Q4, or Q8 (any simple type-only layout) — the question is the post, the design just holds it
- **Caption template:** G1 (This or That), G2 (Tell Me About You), G4 (Complete the Sentence), G6 (What Should I Post), or G9 (Curious)
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + 10 small/medium tags
- **Why this day:** Saturday is comment-day. People scroll with their thumbs up. Make it easy to comment.

### Day 7 — Sunday · Personal

- **Type:** Purely personal
- **Topic:** "What I'm doing today" / "What I read this week" / "A small thing I'm celebrating"
- **Hook:** A scene, not a lesson
- **Post design:** B3 (Meet Me), B7 (Coffee Chat), B10 (Real Talk), or B1 (Founder Story Letter) — no product, no offer, no CTA
- **Caption template:** E3 (What I Wish I Knew) or just a personal, un-captioned post (Instagram's "no caption" era is back)
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + 5-10 small/medium tags
- **Why this day:** Sunday is the pause-day. People are in slow-scrolling mode. Personal posts land soft and deep.

## Week 2 — Push the educational depth

- **Caption template:** E4 (How To Mini-Tutorial) or E7 (Carousel Walkthrough)

- **Why this day:** Monday is a fresh start. A long carousel gives the viewer something to return to mid-week.

### Day 9 — Tuesday · Testimonial

- **Type:** Customer spotlight
- **Topic:** A specific customer's full story — the before, the after, the result
- **Hook:** "Meet [Name]. Six months ago, [problem]. Today, [result]."
- **Post design:** T4 (Before/After) or T9 (Name Plate Review) for the most visual impact; T3 (Portrait + Pull Quote) if you have a headshot
- **Caption template:** E1 (Counterintuitive Take) — frame the testimonial as a lesson, not a brag
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + the matching niche set
- **Why this day:** Trust compounds. Tuesday keeps the trust-building rhythm going.

### Day 10 — Wednesday · Behind-the-Scenes

- **Type:** Tool / process reveal
- **Topic:** "My 6 favorite tools" or "How I [make / shoot / write] every [week / post]"
- **Hook:** "Steal this: the 6 tools that run my entire business."
- **Post design:** B5 (Tools I Use) — 6-photo grid
- **Caption template:** E8 (Steal This) or E2 (Numbered List Drop)
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + 10 small/medium tags
- **Why this day:** Mid-week curiosity. "What's on her desk?" is universal.

### Day 11 — Thursday · Quote / Mantra

- **Type:** Single hero quote — origin story
- **Topic:** A quote from a mentor, a book, a customer, or yourself
- **Hook:** The quote is the post
- **Post design:** Q2 (Top-Anchored Pull Quote) for attributed wisdom; Q6 (Italic Emphasis) for a single-word pivot
- **Caption template:** E3 (What I Wish I Knew) — turn the quote into a story
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + 5-10 small/medium tags

### Day 12 — Friday · Product Promo

- **Type:** Bundle or sale

- **Caption template:** P6 (Bundle / Multi-Buy) or P2 (Sale Announcement) or P7 (Free With Purchase)
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + the matching niche set
- **Why this day:** End-of-week spending. Friday converts.

### Day 13 — Saturday · Engagement

- **Type:** Caption this
- **Topic:** A quirky, candid, behind-the-scenes photo from earlier in the week
- **Hook:** "Caption this. In 5 words or less. Best one gets [the prize]."

- **Caption template:** G7 (Caption This)
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + 10 small/medium tags
- **Why this day:** Saturday = playful. Caption contests are inherently fun.

### Day 14 — Sunday · Personal

- **Type:** Year-in-the-life reflection
- **Topic:** "What this [month / season / year] has taught me" — pick whatever's true
- **Hook:** A specific moment from the period, not a generic "lessons learned" lead

- **Caption template:** E10 (Long Read) or E3 (What I Wish I Knew)
- **Hashtag set:** Add 5 brand tags + 5 small/medium tags
- **Why this day:** Sunday is reflective. A longer read goes deeper.

## How the Buyer Uses This Calendar

1. **Print it. Pin it. Use the same calendar each month.** The order is the point. The rhythm trains your audience to expect a specific type of value on a specific day.
2. **Rotate the *topic*, keep the *type*.** Monday is always Tips. But the topic changes — Week 1 is "3 first-year mistakes," Week 2 is "5-step framework," Week 3 is "myth-bust," Week 4 is "how-to tutorial." The cycle is the engine; the topic is the fuel.
3. **Batch-create 2-3 weeks at a time.** Don't create posts day by day. Sit down once a month, fill in the calendar, then build the posts in batches. This is the single biggest time-saver.
4. **Pair every post with a design + a caption + a hashtag set.** The "Design / Caption / Hashtags" column in the quick-reference is the recipe. Use it as a checklist before hitting publish.
5. **The 5 personal posts (Sundays) are not optional.** They're the trust-builders. A feed that's all tips and promos is a feed that eventually loses to a competitor who's a real person.
6. **The 4 testimonial posts (Tuesdays) are non-negotiable.** They're the trust-builders too. A feed without social proof is a feed that converts at half the rate.
7. **The 4 product posts (Fridays) are the only place you sell.** The other 26 days build the trust that makes Friday convert.
8. **Adjust the niche, not the cycle.** Coaching business? Same calendar. Etsy seller? Same calendar. Photographer? Same calendar. The category rotation is the value. The niche-specific copy is the buyer's job.

## What Changes in Month 2

- **Topics rotate.** Don't repeat the exact same tips/myths/testimonials. Keep the structure, change the subject.
- **Hashtag sets stay.** A 90-day refresh is enough; reusing the same niche set month-over-month is fine.
- **Post designs get re-shuffled.** Use all 10 designs per category across the year. Don't get stuck on the same 3 designs every week.
- **Captions get more confident.** The buyer's voice will tighten as she writes more. By month 3, she'll rewrite the templates in her own voice and won't need the structure anymore.
- **The 30-day cycle is forever.** Don't reinvent the weekly rhythm. Tweak the topics. The rhythm is the moat.
