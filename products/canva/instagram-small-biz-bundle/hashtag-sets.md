# Hashtag Sets — 20 Niches, 15-25 Hashtags Each

Every set is sorted by **size tier**: big (1M+ posts, broad reach), medium (100K-1M posts, niche reach), small (under 100K posts, hyper-targeted community). The mix matters:

- **Big hashtags** (5 per set) put you on a crowded stage. Hard to rank. Use them for discovery.
- **Medium hashtags** (10 per set) are the workhorses. High enough volume to be seen, low enough competition to rank.
- **Small hashtags** (10 per set) are where the real community lives. People who actually engage, save, and follow.

A healthy set has all three tiers. Don't post 30 big tags — you'll drown. Don't post 30 small tags — nobody will find you. The 5/10/10 split is the proven structure.

**Note on size estimates:** The numbers below are directional, not exact. Hashtag counts drift constantly. The relative size is what matters: big tags have millions of posts, small tags have tens of thousands. Use a tool like IQ Hashtags, Flick, or Hashtagstack to refresh counts quarterly.

**Note on usage:** Instagram allows 30 hashtags per post. Each set below has 25. The buyer can add 5 product-specific or brand-specific tags (her own brand tag, a campaign tag, a location tag) to round it out to 30.

**Banned / declining tags:** Never use `#follow`, `#followme`, `#followforfollow`, `#likes`, `#likeforlike`, `#comment`, `#commentbelow`, `#instagood`, `#photooftheday`. They attract bots and signal desperation. They are not in any set below.

## Master Notes for the Buyer

1. **Don't use all 25 in one post unless your account is brand-new.** Use 15-20 per post. Save 5 of the small ones in your bio or for a re-share.
2. **Rotate tiers across posts.** Monday's 5 bigs + 5 mediums, Wednesday's 5 different mediums + 5 different smalls, etc. The point is to surface different parts of the algorithm each time.
3. **Save your top-converting tags.** After 30 days, look at Insights → Post Insights → Reach. The posts that hit the highest reach will share common tags. Promote those tags to your "always include" rotation.
4. **Add 5 brand/product-specific tags every time.** Your own brand tag, a campaign tag, a location tag, a product tag, a series tag. The bundle gives you 25 from the niche; you bring 5 of your own.
5. **Refresh quarterly.** Hashtag sizes drift. A tag that was 50K in June can be 200K by November. Refresh with a tool like Flick or Hashtagstack every 90 days.
6. **Banned tags to never use:** `#follow`, `#followme`, `#followforfollow`, `#likesforlikes`, `#likeforlike`, `#instalike`, `#instagood`, `#photooftheday`, `#picoftheday`, `#commentforcomment`, `#l4l`, `#f4f`. They attract bots and tank reach.
7. **The 30-tag cap is not a target.** It's a ceiling. 15-20 well-chosen tags outperforms 30 stuffed tags on every metric that matters: reach, saves, follows.
