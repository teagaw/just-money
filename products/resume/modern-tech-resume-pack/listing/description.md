## Why This Description Works

### Structure
1. **Hook (first 300 chars)** — Outcome-focused opening that addresses the buyer's pain: "You're qualified. Your resume isn't getting you interviews."
2. **What's Inside** — Scannable list of all 13 deliverables. Buyers can see the value density in 30 seconds.
3. **Why 5 Styles** — Justifies the price ($24 is at the upper end of Etsy resume templates; this section earns it).
4. **3 Formats** — Addresses the buyer's "what tool do I need?" question.
5. **ATS-Passing** — Addresses the buyer's #1 fear (auto-rejection).
6. **Who This Is For** — Self-selection. Buyers who don't fit will self-reject (saving refunds).
7. **How It Works** — Reduces perceived effort. 6 steps, ~30 minutes total.
8. **What Makes This Different** — Justifies the premium price over $5 single-template competitors.
9. **Reviews** — Honest. Empty until real buyers post. No fake quotes.
10. **Pricing & License** — Removes ambiguity.
11. **Questions / Related Products** — Closes with an open invitation + cross-sell.

### What's different from the v1 listing
- **Removed the "Used by 200+ engineers" claim.** It was fabricated and would have been an Etsy/FTC violation.
- **Removed the 3 fake testimonials.** Real reviews get added as they come in.
- **Removed the "[FAANG]" placeholder quote in Variant C.** Self-referential hype is not social proof.
- **Added a "What makes this different" section.** Justifies the price with substance (5 design systems, not 5 color swatches).
- **Changed the candidate description.** "Single candidate Alex Chen" is now stated explicitly and correctly.

### Formatting
- **Use emoji sparingly.** The check marks (✓) and arrows (→) are scannable. The stars (⭐) and dividers (━━━) provide visual structure.
- **Front-load keywords.** "ATS," "resume templates," "software engineers," "PMs," "designers" all appear in the first 1000 characters. Etsy search ranks on description text.
- **Address objections inline.** "Not for: complete beginners" pre-empts refund requests.
- **End with cross-sell.** "Related Products" increases the chance the buyer buys 2 items in the same order.

### Character count
The full description is approximately **4,100 characters** — well under Etsy's 10,000 limit. There's room to add more substance (deeper ATS explainer, ATS safety-rail warning, etc.) as the listing matures.
