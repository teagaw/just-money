## Tag Strategy Notes

### Why these 13?

The buyer personas for this pack are:
- Software engineers (3+ years, applying to tech)
- Product managers (applying to PM roles)
- Designers (applying to design roles at tech companies)
- Founders and operators (exploring the next chapter)
- Senior ICs (staff/principal, applying to senior roles)

The 13 tags are weighted toward the highest-converting segments:
- **4 tags for engineers**: `software engineer`, `tech resume`, `latex resume`, `ATS resume template`
- **2 tags for PMs/designers**: `product manager`, `designer resume`
- **3 tags for style/length**: `modern resume`, `minimalist resume`, `one page resume`
- **2 tags for format/quality**: `resume DOCX`, `professional`
- **2 tags for category/bundle**: `resume template`, `cover letter`

### What we're NOT using (and why)

- ❌ `CV template` — different term, different search intent. The pack is a resume, not a CV (which is the European/Latin American format). Including "CV" would attract non-target buyers.
- ❌ `entry level resume` — the pack is for 3+ years experience. Attracting entry-level buyers leads to refunds.
- ❌ `free resume template` — attracts bargain hunters who won't buy.
- ❌ `creative resume` — too broad. The pack is for tech, not creative industries.
- ❌ `executive resume` — wrong segment. The Executive pack (Q3 2026) will own this term.
- ❌ `infographic resume` — opposite of the design philosophy. The pack is restrained, not infographic.
- ❌ `resume builder` — different product category. We're a template, not a builder.
- ❌ `resume examples` — the pack includes samples, but the primary product is templates.

### A/B testing plan

After 30 days, review Etsy search analytics to see which tags are driving impressions and which are driving clicks. The tags with high impressions but low click-through rate are wasting slot space. Replace them with new candidates.

Common swaps after the first 30 days:
- `founder resume` (if the Founder style starts trending)
- `cover letter template` (re-add if it converts as a bundle)
- `data scientist resume` (if the data segment picks up)
- `y combinator resume` (if the founder/operator segment grows)
- `staff engineer resume` (if the senior-IC segment grows)

### Tag refresh schedule

Etsy re-indexes tags every 7-14 days. The buyer should:
- Review tag performance every 30 days
- Swap 1-2 underperforming tags per review
- Add seasonal tags as relevant (`new grad resume` in May/June, `2026 resume trends` in January)

## Listing Attribute Fields (in addition to tags)

Etsy has additional attribute fields beyond the 13 tags. The buyer should fill in all of them:

| Field | Recommended value |
|-------|-------------------|
| Category | Paper & Party Supplies > Paper > Stationery > Resume |
| Recipient | Unisex, Men, Women, Teens |
| Occasion | Career, Graduation, New Job |
| Style | Modern, Minimalist, Professional, Bold, Technical |
| Material | Digital Download |
| Color | Multicolor |
| Size | Letter (8.5 x 11 in) |
| Personalization | Yes (the buyer can request color customization) |

These attributes don't directly affect search ranking, but they affect the buyer's filter experience. Filling them out gets the listing into more filtered search results.
