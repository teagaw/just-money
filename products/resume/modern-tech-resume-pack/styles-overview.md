# The 5 Styles — Overview

Five distinct visual systems, each engineered for a specific career, hiring manager, and recruiter skim path. Pick the one that matches how you want to be read.

---

## At a Glance

| # | Style | Vibe | Best For | ATS Path | Color |
|---|-------|------|----------|----------|-------|
| 1 | The Minimalist | Quiet, confident, generous | Senior+ ICs, staff, principal, directors | Single column | None (black/white) |
| 2 | The Modern | Balanced, structured, friendly | Product managers, analysts, hybrid roles | Single column + sidebar | Muted blue or sage |
| 3 | The Bold | Confident, eye-catching, refined | Designers, brand-side, creative tech | Single column with hero | Burnt orange or oxblood |
| 4 | The Engineer | Technical, precise, no-nonsense | Backend, infra, ML, systems, security | Single column | Charcoal + 1 mono accent |
| 5 | The Founder | Dense, story-driven, multi-venture | Founders, execs, advisors, operators | Single column with stacked sections | Black + 1 brand color |

---

## 1. The Minimalist

**One sentence:** *The resume of someone who has nothing to prove.*

**Vibe:** Quietly confident. Lots of whitespace. No color. No decoration. The document is doing the same work a well-tailored suit does — disappearing so the substance shows.

**Visual signature:**
- Generous 0.75" margins all around
- Name in 28pt serif (Garamond, Cormorant, or Charter) or a refined sans (Inter Tight, Söhne)
- Body in 10.5pt sans (Inter, Söhne, Helvetica Neue)
- Section headings in small caps, letter-spaced, 9pt
- Single column, full-width
- Optional hairline rule (0.5pt) under the name only
- No icons. No boxes. No color. No skill bars.

**Best for:**
- Staff engineers, principal engineers, senior+ ICs
- Directors, VPs, distinguished engineers
- Senior designers with 10+ years of brand work
- Anyone whose career *is* the proof

**Recruiter skim path:** Reads like a New Yorker essay. The whitespace tells the reader the candidate is senior enough to need no ornament.

**Pairing:** Cover letter uses the same restraint — short paragraphs, no opener filler, leading with a specific claim.

## 2. The Modern

**One sentence:** *The resume of someone who balances five things at once.*

**Vibe:** Structured but not rigid. A muted color in the sidebar adds warmth without distraction. This is the universal professional template — the one that works in 90% of tech interviews.

**Visual signature:**
- Left sidebar (1.6" wide) in muted color block (off-white, pale blue, sage)
- Name in 22pt sans, white-on-color header band
- Body in 10pt sans
- Section headings in 11pt semibold, accent color
- Sidebar contains: contact, skills, tools, languages, certifications
- Main column contains: summary, experience, projects, education
- Rounded corners on the sidebar (subtle 4px)

**Best for:**
- Product managers
- Product designers (less visual than The Bold)
- Marketing & growth leaders
- Data scientists who work cross-functionally
- Hybrid IC/lead roles

**Recruiter skim path:** Eye lands on the colored sidebar first, scans skills in 4 seconds, then drops into the experience column for proof.

**Pairing:** Cover letter uses the same two-column rhythm with a left margin note column.

---

## 3. The Bold

**One sentence:** *The resume of someone whose taste is the product.*

**Vibe:** Confident color. Asymmetric layout. The candidate treats their resume as a design artifact. Refined, not loud.

**Visual signature:**
- Full-bleed hero band at top (1.2") in burnt orange, oxblood, or forest green
- Name in 32pt sans-serif, white-on-color, slightly off-center
- 1pt hairline divider in accent color between sections
- Body in 10pt sans
- Section headings in 14pt semibold, accent color, slightly oversized
- Generous left margin (1"), tighter right (0.5") — intentional asymmetry
- Optional small accent mark (◆, ▣, or —) before each role
- Project section uses 2-column grid for thumbnails (optional)

**Best for:**
- Senior product designers
- Brand designers
- Creative directors
- Design engineers (front-end with strong design sense)
- Founders in consumer / brand / design-led spaces

**Recruiter skim path:** The hero band earns 2 seconds of attention. Asymmetric layout signals taste. Eye drops to the experience and the candidate's portfolio link.

**Pairing:** Cover letter mirrors the hero band — short paragraphs, a single bold line opener, and the rest reads like a polished note.

## 4. The Engineer

**One sentence:** *The resume of someone who ships infrastructure.*

**Vibe:** Terminal-clean. Monospace accents. No decoration. The visual system signals: "I read code, I write code, I do not waste your time."

**Visual signature:**
- Off-white background (#FAFAF7) or pure white
- Name in 18pt monospace (JetBrains Mono, Fira Code, Berkeley Mono) or 22pt sans with mono accents
- Section headings in 10pt UPPERCASE monospace, letter-spaced
- Body in 10pt sans (Inter, IBM Plex Sans)
- Bullet markers are `▸` or `—` not `•`
- Inline code in monospace (e.g., `Python`, `PostgreSQL`, `Kafka`)
- No icons, no boxes, no color
- Optional thin mono divider `────` between major sections
- URLs in mono, no underline, just color shift (charcoal, not blue)

**Best for:**
- Backend engineers (distributed systems, infra, platform)
- ML / AI engineers
- Security engineers
- Data engineers
- Database engineers
- SRE / platform engineers

**Recruiter skim path:** Tech leads and EM reviewers see the mono and read the resume longer. HR screeners see the structure and pass it through ATS cleanly.

**Pairing:** Cover letter uses a single-column mono-and-sans rhythm — no opener fluff, leads with a specific technical claim, ends with a clear ask.

## 5. The Founder

**One sentence:** *The resume of someone who has built multiple things.*

**Vibe:** Dense. Story-driven. Multi-section. The document reads like a portfolio index — ventures, exits, advisory roles, press, side projects, talks. It is not a CV; it is a one-page operating record.

**Visual signature:**
- Single column, full width
- Name in 24pt semibold sans, followed by a 1-line headline ("Founder & CEO, Acme • ex-Stripe • YC W19")
- Body in 9.5pt sans (slightly smaller — the density is the point)
- Section headings in 11pt UPPERCASE letter-spaced sans
- Custom sections: **Ventures**, **Operating Experience**, **Advisory & Board**, **Press & Talks**, **Side Projects**
- Each role entry: company, role, dates, 1-line outcome, 2-3 bullet proof points
- Uses compact date formatting (2021—Present)
- Optional small logos section (when sent as PDF — text fallback for ATS)

**Best for:**
- Founders and co-founders
- Exited founders exploring the next thing
- Operators and execs (CPO, CTO, COO at growth-stage)
- Senior advisors and board members
- VCs moving to operating roles
- Anyone whose career does not fit a single "title"

**Recruiter skim path:** The headline tells the story in one line. The Ventures section is the proof. Press & Talks is the social proof. Side Projects signals the founder is still building.

**Pairing:** Cover letter is conversational, leads with a specific point of view, and avoids the "I am excited to apply" opener entirely.

## How to Choose

Answer two questions:

1. **What is the dominant signal you want to send in 3 seconds?**
   - Seniority and restraint → Minimalist
   - Balance and structure → Modern
   - Taste and confidence → Bold
   - Technical depth → Engineer
   - Range and ownership → Founder

2. **Who reads the resume first?**
   - An IC hiring manager (staff eng, design lead) → Minimalist or Engineer
   - A recruiter doing a phone screen → Modern
   - A design-aware HM (creative director, founder) → Bold
   - A tech lead or EM → Engineer
   - A board member or VC → Founder

If still unsure, default to **The Modern** — it is the safest choice for a first-time tech job application. Once you have a target company, switch to the style that mirrors their culture.

## ATS Compatibility — Universal Rules

All 5 styles pass ATS by following these rules:

1. **Single column** for the parse path. Two-column layouts (The Modern, The Bold hero) use **mirrored single-column fallbacks** for ATS submission. The buyer uses the visual version for the human, the ATS version for the bot.
2. **Standard section headings** exactly: `Experience`, `Education`, `Skills`. Synonyms break parsers.
3. **No tables, no text boxes, no graphics, no icons** in the ATS version.
4. **No headers or footers** in the ATS version. ATS skips these.
5. **Standard fonts only** in the ATS version: Arial, Calibri, Helvetica, Times New Roman, Garamond.
6. **.docx submission** for ATS — PDF for human readers (PDF preserves formatting across systems but some legacy ATS only parse DOCX).
7. **Filename:** `FirstName-LastName-Resume-2026.docx` — not "resume-final-v3".

See `ats-guide.md` for the full technical breakdown.

## File Outputs Per Style

For each of the 5 styles, the pack ships:

- `1-page.docx` — editable in Word & Google Docs
- `1-page.tex` — editable in LaTeX (Overleaf, TeXShop)
- `1-page-ats.docx` — stripped, single-column ATS-only version
- `2-page.docx` — bonus for senior candidates
- `2-page.tex`
- `sample-filled.md` — fully written example resume
- `preview.png` — rendered visual preview

That's **7 files × 5 styles = 35 template files** in the final pack, plus the 5 sample resumes in editable DOCX/PDF.
