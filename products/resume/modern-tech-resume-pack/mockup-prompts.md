# Mockup Prompts — 5 Styles

**Use:** Secondary listing images that show each of the 5 styles in a real-world context. Etsy allows up to 10 images; Creative Market up to 12; Gumroad unlimited. Use 5 mockups minimum, one per style.

Each style gets its own mockup so the buyer can see the actual product before purchasing. The mockups are what convert browsers into buyers.

---

## Mockup 1 — The Minimalist

**Goal:** Convey restraint, whitespace, and senior-level confidence.

```
A premium editorial top-down photograph of a single US Letter resume on a clean off-white
linen surface, the resume showing "ALEX CHEN" in elegant serif typography (Garamond or
Cormorant), 28pt, with a single 0.5pt hairline rule below the name, body text in clean
sans-serif with generous margins, no color anywhere on the resume, no decoration, lots of
intentional whitespace filling 40% of the page, soft natural window light from the upper
left, a single brass letter opener resting in the lower right corner of the frame, no other
objects, no logos, no watermarks, editorial product photography aesthetic, 35mm film grain,
neutral palette of cream and charcoal, Hasselblad-style, 8K detail, photorealistic
--ar 4:5 --style raw --v 6.1
```

**What this mockup demonstrates:** The whitespace. The buyer can see that the page is 40% empty — that's the design.

**Post-processing:** Slight vignette to draw the eye. Verify "ALEX CHEN" renders correctly.

## Post-Processing Workflow (All Mockups)

1. **Verify text rendering.** AI image generators often garble text. Re-roll or composite the text in Photoshop.
2. **Color correct** in Lightroom. The neutral palette should feel premium, not yellow or blue-shifted.
3. **Add a subtle vignette** to draw the eye to the resume.
4. **Crop to the marketplace's required dimensions:**
   - Etsy: 270 × 270 minimum for thumbnails, 2000 × 2000 recommended for zoom
   - Creative Market: 1500 × 1500 minimum, 2400 × 2400 recommended
   - Gumroad: 1280 × 720 for the cover, 600 × 600 for thumbnails
   - Own site: 1200 × 1200 minimum, 2400 × 2400 for retina
5. **Export as PNG** for lossless, or JPG at 95% quality for smaller files.
6. **Add the pack's branding** as a small watermark or wordmark — typically lower right corner, max 5% of the image area.

## Common Pitfalls

- ❌ **AI generators adding fake text** that doesn't match the design. Re-roll, or composite the real text in Photoshop.
- ❌ **Too many props.** The resume is the focal point. One pen, one notebook, one plant. Not all three.
- ❌ **Brand watermarks** that distract. No Apple logos, no obvious brand cues.
- ❌ **Saturated colors.** Premium = muted. Burnt orange should look like terracotta, not traffic cone.
- ❌ **Identical compositions across mockups.** Each style should have its own visual context. The Minimalist = linen + letter opener. The Engineer = linen + keyboard. The Founder = linen + notebook.
- ❌ **Visible model faces** in the lifestyle shots. The hands should be neutral, the focus on the resume.
