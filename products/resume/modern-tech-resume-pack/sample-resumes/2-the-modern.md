# Sample Resume — The Modern (Alex Chen)

A fully filled-in example for **Style #2: The Modern**.
Use this as a reference for tone, density, and the two-column sidebar rhythm. The visual version is `deliverables/the-modern.tex` and the swappable content is `deliverables/the-modern-content.md`.

**Note on the candidate:** All 5 sample resumes in this pack use the **same** Alex Chen — Staff Software Engineer, 9 years, the canonical 5-company career (Stripe → Cloudflare → Dropbox → Google → Microsoft). The Modern style is showcased here with Alex's **cross-functional technical-lead work** as the framing: a Staff SWE partnering with PM, Design, and Data Science. If you are a PM or Designer using The Modern, replace the sidebar's "Core Skills" with your own stack — see `the-modern-content.md` for the PM/Designer/Analyst sidebar swaps.

## Reading Notes for the Buyer

**Why this works for a SWE (not just PMs):** The Modern style works for any technical IC. The sidebar's "Core Skills" here are engineering skills (Distributed Systems, Architecture, SLO Design), and the main column emphasizes **cross-functional leadership** — the same canonical career told with a PM-adjacent lens. If you are a non-engineer, swap the sidebar as documented in `the-modern-content.md`.

**Density:** 1 page, ~520 words. Sidebar contains ~140 words. Main column contains ~380 words. The sidebar is information-dense but not crowded — readable in 6 seconds.

**Sidebar logic:** The sidebar answers the *first question* a recruiter asks: "Does this person have the stack?" Skills at the top, tools below, languages and interests tertiary. If a recruiter reads nothing else, they read the skills.

**Main column logic:** The main column tells the *second question*: "Has this person shipped AND partnered cross-functionally?" The first Stripe bullet shows cross-functional ownership; the bullets after show technical depth.

**The single-candidate promise:** This is the **same Alex Chen** you see in the Minimalist, Bold, Engineer, and Founder samples. The only thing that changes between styles is the framing — Modern emphasizes cross-functional leadership, Minimalist emphasizes impact metrics, Bold emphasizes side projects, Engineer emphasizes system+tech+scale+outcome, Founder emphasizes the side venture.

**What the buyer should change:**
- The sidebar's "Core Skills" and "Tools & Platforms" to your own stack
- The summary to your own role + a specific outcome you're proud of + what you're looking for next
- For each experience bullet, include the team size and cross-functional partners — engineers ship through other people too
- For tools: pick the 8-10 you actually use, not the "nice to have" ones

**What the buyer should NOT change:**
- The sidebar/main column split — this is the design
- The section order in the sidebar (Contact → Links → Skills → Tools → Languages → Interests)
- The color variant unless they have a specific reason (default is muted blue)
- The header band — it is the visual identity
- The right-aligned dates in the experience section

**Color notes:** This sample uses the muted blue variant. To switch to sage green, change `modAccent` from `2C5F7F` to `5A7A5E` and `modBg` from `EAF1F4` to `EEF1EC` in the LaTeX file. The compile takes about 8 seconds on Overleaf.
