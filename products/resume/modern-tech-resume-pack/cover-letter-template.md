# Cover Letter Template — The Modern (default pairing)

**Default pairing:** The Modern (Style #2). Also works paired with The Minimalist, The Engineer, or The Founder.
**Length:** 3 paragraphs, ~250 words.
**Tone:** Direct, specific, no opener filler, no closer fluff.

The cover letter that ships in the pack is matched to The Modern's two-column rhythm. The buyer's resume shows the *what*; the cover letter explains the *why this role, why this company, why now*.

## Why This Structure Works

### Paragraph 1: Lead with a specific claim
Not "I am excited to apply." Not "I am writing to express my interest." Not "I am a passionate engineer with X years of experience."

The first sentence states who the buyer is, what they've done, and why they're writing. Three facts. No adjectives.

### Paragraph 2: The "three things" pivot
Lists are easier to read than prose. Three numbered points is the right density — two is too few, four is too many. Each point answers: *what have I done that proves I can do this job?*

### Paragraph 3: Why this role, why now
The buyer states the *level* they want to operate at and *what they're ready to do*. This signals ambition without arrogance. The HM reads it and thinks: "This person has thought about their next chapter."

### Closer: One sentence, no fluff
"I can start the conversation whenever works." Direct. No "I look forward to hearing from you." No "Thank you for your time and consideration." The cover letter ends on a logistical line, which signals confidence.

## Buyer Customization Checklist

- [ ] Replace bracketed fields with real values
- [ ] Lead with the most specific claim from your actual career
- [ ] Pick the 3 things that are most relevant to *this* role, not your entire career
- [ ] Reference the specific job posting language — but paraphrase, do not copy
- [ ] Add the company's name once in the opening and once in the closer
- [ ] Mention the office location if you are local (it's a small but real signal)
- [ ] Save as PDF (not DOCX) for email submission
- [ ] Filename: `FirstName-LastName-CoverLetter-CompanyName.pdf`

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

❌ "I am writing to express my strong interest in the [Role] position at [Company]."
✅ "I'm the [title] who [did the thing]. I'm writing because [specific reason]."

❌ "I am a passionate, driven, results-oriented engineer with a proven track record of..."
✅ "I've [done the specific thing the JD asks for]. I have the scars from the [specific situation]."

❌ "I would love the opportunity to discuss how my background aligns with your needs."
✅ "I can start the conversation whenever works."

❌ "Thank you for your time and consideration."
✅ (Delete. End on a logistical line.)

❌ "Please find my resume attached."
✅ (Delete. The HM has your resume. They know.)

## Style Pairings

The default cover letter uses The Modern's two-column visual rhythm. If the buyer is using a different resume style, the cover letter adjusts:

- **The Minimalist resume** → Cover letter is single-column, 0.75" margins, serif name. Match the restraint.
- **The Modern resume** → Cover letter is the default above. Two-column rhythm.
- **The Bold resume** → Cover letter has a colored hero band with the name, asymmetric margins. Match the confidence.
- **The Engineer resume** → Cover letter uses mono accents, `//` prefix on the role reference, mono for tech names. Match the precision.
- **The Founder resume** → Cover letter is denser, has a one-line headline at the top, may include a "Why this company" mini-section. Match the range.

The pack ships the Modern default plus 4 style-matched variants as separate files.

## Length Calibration

| Career stage | Cover letter length |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Entry-level (0-3 yrs) | 1 page, 4 paragraphs, ~350 words |
| Mid-level (3-7 yrs) | 0.75 page, 3 paragraphs, ~250 words (default) |
| Senior (7-12 yrs) | 0.5 page, 3 paragraphs, ~200 words |
| Executive / Founder | 0.5 page, 2 paragraphs, ~150 words |

The default ships at 250 words for mid-to-senior. Buyers should adjust down for senior, up for entry-level.
