**Volume-counting convention (used throughout):** Volume = weight × reps × sets. Unilateral moves (Bulgarian split squat, walking lunge) count **one leg only** with **one dumbbell**. Cable/machine work counts the stack weight, not bodyweight. Calf raise counts the *added* weight on a machine (not bodyweight). This convention is consistent across all 4 weeks — so weekly volume trends are directly comparable.
