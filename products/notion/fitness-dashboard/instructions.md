## ⚡ 5-Minute Quickstart

> Do these four things, in order, and you're training. Save the rest of this document for later.

### 1. Duplicate the template (60 seconds)

1. Open the Notion link from your delivery email.
2. Click **Duplicate** in the top-right corner.
3. Save the copy to your personal workspace.

That's it. You now own the dashboard.

### 2. Replace the welcome callout (30 seconds)

On the Home page, click the welcome callout and change `[Name]` to your name. Skip the rest for now.

The Home page has a **🚧 Show Sample Data** toggle. Turn it **OFF** if you want a clean slate, leave it **ON** for the first 2 weeks so you can see what filled-in entries look like.

1. Click **🏋️ Workouts** in the sidebar.
2. Hit **+ New** (or click today's date on the calendar).
3. Fill in: Session title, Date, Program, Duration. (Energy + Notes are optional.)
4. Save.

**That's the whole system in 5 minutes.** The rest of this document goes deeper — when you're ready, start at **Step 1** below.

## Step 2: Customize the Basics (10 minutes)

### 2.1 Set Your Name and Goal

On the Home page, click the Welcome callout and replace:
- `[Name]` with your name
- The "Current Streak" callout is auto-pulled from the Workouts database (no edit needed)
- The "Next Scheduled" callout: edit to reflect your training plan (e.g., "Wed — Upper A")

### 2.2 Pick Your Program

Open the **📚 Programs** page. You'll see three options:

| Program | Days/Week | Best For |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| Beginner Full Body | 3 | First 6 months of training |
| Intermediate Upper/Lower | 4 | 6-24 months of consistent training |
| Advanced Push/Pull/Legs | 5 | 2+ years, want hypertrophy or strength focus |

Choose the one that matches your current level. **When in doubt, go one level down.** You can always graduate up; starting too advanced leads to form breakdown and injury.

### 2.3 Set Up Your First Goal

Open **🎯 Goals**. Click "+ New" and create at least one goal. Use the SAMPLE entries as a template.

A good first goal follows the SMART pattern:
- **Specific:** "Bench press 80 kg for 1 rep" (not "get stronger")
- **Measurable:** the weight is the metric
- **Achievable:** ~10-20% above your current 1RM
- **Relevant:** it matters to you
- **Time-bound:** "by August 15"

Create 2-4 goals. The dashboard supports more, but 2-4 is the sweet spot for focus.

### 2.4 Configure Your Daily Habits

Open **🔁 Daily Habits**. The sample has 5 habits. Customize them to your life:

- **Keep:** any habit that genuinely moves the needle (steps, protein, sleep)
- **Add:** 1-2 habits that are personal to you (meditation, hydration, reading)
- **Delete:** any you don't care about (the sample includes one to demo the system)

**Limit to 3-5 daily habits.** More than that and the consistency % becomes demoralizing.

## Step 3: Your First Workout Log (15 minutes)

### 3.1 Open 🏋️ Workouts

You'll see the Calendar view. Click the **+ New** button or click any date on the calendar.

### 3.2 Fill Out the Workout Entry

For a typical strength session:

| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Session (title) | "Mon — Upper A" |
| Date | Today's date |
| Program | Intermediate Upper A |
| Type | Strength, Hypertrophy |
| Duration (min) | 52 |
| Avg RPE | 7 |
| Energy Before | 4 Med-High |
| Energy After | 4 Med-High |
| Notes | "Felt strong. Bench moved easy." |

**Most fields are optional.** The must-fills are: Session, Date, Program. Everything else is for richness.

### 3.3 Add Linked Exercises

In the **Linked Exercises** property, click and search for exercises like "Bench Press" or create new ones.

For each exercise, fill in:
- Set 1 — Weight (kg): 65
- Set 1 — Reps: 6
- (repeat for each set)
- Notes / coaching cues: optional

The **Estimated 1RM** and **Volume (kg)** formulas auto-calculate. The **Total Volume** on the workout rolls up from all linked exercises.

### 3.4 Save and Watch the Dashboard Update

Hit save. Open the **📊 Home** page. Your "Last Workout" callout has updated. Your weekly volume has updated. The streak counter has updated.

This is the magic moment — the dashboard becomes alive.

## Step 4: Daily Logging Ritual (5 minutes/day)

Once the dashboard is set up, your daily ritual is:

### Morning (2 minutes)
1. Open **😴 Recovery** → log last night's sleep and quality
2. Open **🔁 Daily Habits** → check off what you did yesterday (yes, yesterday — it's a habit *log*, not a habit plan)

### Workout Days (5-10 minutes, post-workout)
1. Open **🏋️ Workouts** → log the session
2. Add linked exercises
3. (Optional) Add a note about how it felt

### Meal Logging (3 minutes, with each meal OR once at end of day)
- Open **🍽️ Nutrition Log** → add a meal entry
- Calorie/macro fields are optional. Many users log only protein and call it a day.

### Weekly Check-in (10 minutes, every Sunday or Monday)
1. Open **📏 Body Metrics** → log weight, waist, optional measurements
2. Open **📈 Weekly Review** toggle on Home → write 3 wins, 3 lessons, next week's focus
3. Look at the **This Week's Snapshot** on Home — are the workouts, macros, and recovery aligned?

## Pro Tips (From Iterating on the Design)

### Tip 1: Don't Log Everything, Log What Matters

The dashboard supports 30+ properties across 8 databases. **You don't need to fill them all.** The minimum-viable log:
- Workout: title, date, program, duration, RPE
- Exercise: weight × reps for your top set
- Nutrition: calories and protein (ignore micros for the first 3 months)
- Body Metrics: weight, waist (weekly)
- Recovery: sleep hours
- Habits: yes/no check

Everything else is for when you're ready to optimize.

### Tip 2: Set Up a "Trigger" — Pair Logging with an Existing Habit

The biggest failure mode is forgetting to log. Pair it with something you already do:
- **"After I finish a workout, I open Workouts before I leave the gym"** (do it in the locker room)
- **"After I brush my teeth at night, I check off Daily Habits"**
- **"After I weigh myself on Monday morning, I open Body Metrics"**

Stack the new habit on top of the old one. Logging becomes automatic in 2-3 weeks.

### Tip 3: The Weekly Review is the Highest-Leverage Page

Most users underweight the **📈 Weekly Review** toggle. It's the page that turns raw data into insight.

A good weekly review takes 10 minutes and answers:
- What worked this week?
- What didn't?
- What will I focus on next week?

Do it consistently for 8 weeks and you'll have a clearer picture of your training than 95% of gym-goers.

### Tip 4: The Photos are the Most Powerful Part

Progress photos are awkward to take and easy to skip. Take them anyway. After 12 weeks, when you scroll back to Week 0, you'll see what the scale couldn't tell you. The "Comparison" view in the Progress Photos database makes this visceral.

**Tip:** Take photos at the same time of day, same lighting, same angle. Front + Side + Back. Same outfit (or no outfit — your call). Store them privately; the dashboard has a "Hidden from Home Gallery" privacy checkbox for each entry.

### Tip 5: Don't Obsess Over Daily Numbers

Weight fluctuates 1-2 kg day-to-day from water, food volume, and glycogen. The **7-Day Rolling Avg** in the Weekly Review is more useful than the daily number. Same for sleep, soreness, mood — patterns matter, single days don't.

### Tip 6: The Recovery Score is a Permission Slip

If your Recovery Score is below 50 for 2+ consecutive days, the dashboard is telling you: rest. Take a deload. Don't push through. The formula is in the Recovery database — read it, trust it.

### Tip 7: Use the Mobile App

The Notion mobile app is actually good. Pin the dashboard to your home screen. Logging a meal takes 30 seconds from your phone. Doing a quick workout log in the locker room takes 60 seconds. The friction is low if you let it be.

### Tip 8: Back Up Quarterly

Notion doesn't have a "real" backup feature. Every 3 months:
1. Open the dashboard
2. Hit the "..." menu → "Export"
3. Choose "Markdown & CSV" or "PDF"
4. Save to a folder in Google Drive / iCloud

This protects you from accidental deletions, Notion outages, or rare sync issues.

## Customization Ideas

Once you've used the dashboard for 2-4 weeks, you'll want to customize it. Here are common additions:

### Add a New Database

**Example: Supplement Stack**
- Properties: Name, Dose, Frequency, Cost, Notes, Linked Goal
- Use: track what you're taking, why, and what it costs

**Example: Gym Log (Travel Days)**
- Properties: City, Gym Name, Address, Equipment Notes, Rating
- Use: remember good gyms when you travel

**Example: PR Board**
- Properties: Lift, Weight, Date, Video, Notes
- Use: a leaderboard view of your top lifts, sorted by date

### Add a New View to an Existing Database

**Example: Workouts — "This Month"**
- View type: Table
- Filter: Date is within "this month"
- Sort: Date (newest first)
- Show: Session, Date, Total Volume, Avg RPE, Notes

**Example: Exercises — "Upper Body Only"**
- View type: Gallery
- Filter: Muscle Group contains "Chest" OR "Back" OR "Shoulders" OR "Biceps" OR "Triceps"

### Add a New Formula

**Example: Workouts — "Days Since Last Session"**
- Formula: `dateBetween(now(), prop("Date"), "days")`
- Use: a column that shows 0, 1, 2, 3... days since the workout

**Example: Body Metrics — "BMI"**
- Formula: `round(prop("Weight") / (1.75 * 1.75) * 10) / 10`  (replace 1.75 with your height in meters)
- Use: a quick BMI calc, even though BMI is a flawed metric, trends can be useful

## Troubleshooting

### Formulas Showing "NaN" or Errors

**Cause:** Division by zero or empty property referenced.

**Fix:** Wrap the formula in a conditional:
- Original: `prop("X") / prop("Y")`
- Fixed: `if(prop("Y") == 0, 0, prop("X") / prop("Y"))`

### Linked Database View on Home Shows Nothing

**Cause:** The source database is empty (you deleted all sample data and haven't logged anything yet).

**Fix:** Either add an entry, or temporarily show sample data via the toggle.

### Mobile Sidebar Order is Wrong

**Cause:** Notion doesn't let you drag sub-pages into a specific order on mobile. The order is set on desktop.

**Fix:** On desktop, drag the sub-pages in the sidebar into the order specified in `template-spec.md` §1.1. The order syncs to mobile.

### The Recovery Score Formula Looks Wrong

**Cause:** You may have edited the formula and broken it.

**Fix:** The correct formula is in the Recovery database, Recovery Score property. Reset to:
```
if(empty(prop("Sleep")), 0, (prop("Sleep") * 10) + if(prop("Sleep Quality") == "Great", 30, if(prop("Sleep Quality") == "Good", 20, if(prop("Sleep Quality") == "OK", 10, 0))) + if(prop("Soreness") == "None", 30, if(prop("Soreness") == "Mild", 20, if(prop("Soreness") == "Moderate", 10, 0))) - if(prop("Stress") == "High", 20, if(prop("Stress") == "Med-High", 10, 0)))
```

(If you want to cap the score at 100 even when sleep is unusually high, wrap the whole right-hand side in `min(100, …)`. The un-capped version lets the score exceed 100 on truly excellent recovery days, which the Home dashboard then displays verbatim.)

### I Lost the Home Dashboard

**Cause:** Accidentally deleted or moved.

**Fix:** Every top-level page in Notion can be restored from "Trash" within 30 days. Workspace settings → Trash → find "📊 Fitness Dashboard" → Restore.

### The Template Doesn't Match My Goal

**Fix:** Goals are not template-locked. Edit any goal, add new ones, delete ones that don't resonate. The architecture is yours.

## What to Do When You Outgrow the Dashboard

After 6-12 months of consistent use, you may want to:

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| You're a competitive powerlifter | Migrate to a spreadsheet (this dashboard's Volume and 1RM formulas export cleanly to CSV) |
| You hired a coach | Toggle "Coach Mode" on Home, share the read-only link with them |
| You want to add wearable integration | Use Notion's API + Zapier to auto-import Whoop/Oura data into the Recovery database |
| You want a custom app | The data exports to CSV — you have a clean dataset to build on |

**The dashboard is yours forever.** Use it as long as it serves you, then export the data and move on. No subscriptions, no lock-in.
