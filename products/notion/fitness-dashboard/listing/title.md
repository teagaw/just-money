# Listing Title — Etsy / Gumroad

> Etsy allows 140 characters. Gumroad allows 60-80. We optimize for Etsy's longer limit (it indexes better in search) and provide a shorter variant for Gumroad.

---

## Primary Title (Etsy — 139 characters)

```
Notion Fitness Dashboard Template, Workout Tracker + Gym Planner + Meal Log + Body Metrics, Aesthetic Notion Health OS
```

**Character count:** 139 / 140

### Why This Works

**Front-loads the highest-volume keyword:** "Notion Fitness Dashboard" is the primary search term buyers use. Putting it first matches buyer's exact search query.

**Stacks 5 long-tail keywords:**
- "Notion Fitness Dashboard Template" — exact match
- "Workout Tracker" — top related search
- "Gym Planner" — second top related
- "Meal Log" — niche long-tail
- "Body Metrics" — niche long-tail
- "Aesthetic Notion Health OS" — branded long-tail for the "all-in-one OS" crowd

**Avoids banned words:** Etsy penalizes titles with "Best," "Top," "Free," "Sale," "Discount," all-caps spam, special characters that aren't letters.

**Doesn't include price** in the title (Etsy displays price separately; including it doesn't help SEO).

**Readable by humans AND machines:** A buyer scanning search results sees "Notion Fitness Dashboard" and knows exactly what they're getting.

## Variation A (Etsy — 138 characters, more emotional)

```
Notion Fitness Dashboard for Beginners, Aesthetic Workout Planner with Meal Log, Body Metrics & Recovery Tracker
```

**Character count:** 138 / 140

### Why This Works

Targets the "beginner" segment specifically — a high-volume, high-conversion sub-niche. Includes "Aesthetic" (top aesthetic-search modifier) and lists every major feature.

## Variation B (Etsy — 137 characters, more premium)

```
Premium Notion Fitness Dashboard, 8 Databases, 12 Formulas, Sample Data, Mobile Friendly, Workout Meal Recovery
```

**Character count:** 137 / 140

### Why This Works

Leads with "Premium" (positions above the $9-12 noise), quantifies the value (8 databases, 12 formulas — buyers love specificity), and lists the use cases at the end.

## Gumroad Title (shorter, 70 characters)

```
Premium Notion Fitness Dashboard — Plan, Track, Crush
```

**Character count:** 70 / 80

### Why This Works

Gumroad titles are shorter because the product page itself has a long-form description. The "Plan, Track, Crush" is the tagline from the listing description, gives it personality.

## TikTok / Instagram / X Caption Variant (60 characters)

```
The Notion dashboard that actually makes you train consistently.
```

**Character count:** 59 / 60 (TikTok caption limit)

### Why This Works

Lead with the *outcome* (train consistently) not the *thing* (Notion template). Captures attention in 2 seconds. Use as the hook in any social post.

```
Notion Fitness Dashboard Template — Aesthetic Workout & Meal Planner
```

**Character count:** 100 / 100

Pinterest titles can be 100 chars. Front-loads the search term, includes "Aesthetic" (top Pinterest search modifier), and lists the two most-pinned features (Workout + Meal Planner).

## A/B Testing Recommendation

**Week 1-2:** Run **Primary Title** on Etsy. This is the safest, most SEO-optimized option.

**Week 3-4:** If impressions are high but conversion is low, switch to **Variation A** (beginner-focused). Beginners convert higher because the value is clearer.

**Week 5+:** If you have a "premium" customer segment forming (people buying the $19 vs the $9 alternatives), switch to **Variation B**.

**Track:** Impressions, click-through rate, conversion rate, average review score. The right title is the one that converts your specific traffic, not the one with the most keywords.

## Banned-Word Checklist (for Etsy's Algorithm)

Etsy's search algorithm penalizes:
- ❌ "Best," "Top," "#1," "Cheapest"
- ❌ "Free," "Discount," "Sale," "% off"
- ❌ ALL CAPS words (use Title Case, not SHOUTING)
- ❌ Special characters: ★ ♥ ✦ ❤
- ❌ Promotional language: "Buy now," "Limited time"
- ❌ Brand names of competitors: "Better than [Competitor]"

Our title contains none of these. ✅
