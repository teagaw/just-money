## Why These Specific Tags

### Primary Keywords (high volume, high intent)

- **notion template** — every Notion listing on Etsy uses this; required for category visibility
- **notion fitness** — the broad category, high volume
- **notion workout** — the action verb, captures "I want to track workouts"
- **fitness template** — cross-platform keyword, captures non-Notion-aware buyers
- **notion tracker** — captures the tracking-intent buyer
- **notion planner** — captures the planning-intent buyer
- **notion dashboard** — captures the "all-in-one" buyer

### Niche Long-Tails (lower volume, lower competition, higher conversion)

- **notion gym** — gym-specific
- **notion meal planner** — nutrition-specific
- **notion health** — health-aware buyer
- **notion aesthetic** — design-conscious buyer
- **notion OS** — the "operating system" mindset buyer
- **notion goals** — goal-tracking buyer

This mix covers both the high-volume generic searches AND the specific long-tail searches. Etsy ranks listings on **relevance + recency + reviews + tags**, so having both generic and specific tags helps you rank on both.

---

## Tag Selection Strategy

**Do NOT use:**
- ❌ Tags longer than 20 characters
- ❌ Tags with special characters (`★`, `&`, `#`)
- ❌ Multi-word phrases with stop words ("the", "and", "of")
- ❌ Competitor brand names
- ❌ Irrelevant trending tags (Etsy will penalize for non-relevance)
- ❌ All 13 tags being the same word with minor variations

**DO use:**
- ✅ A mix of high-volume and long-tail tags
- ✅ Tags that match what buyers actually type into search
- ✅ Tags that are honest descriptors of your product
- ✅ A mix of single-word and multi-word tags
- ✅ Tags that align with your title keywords (Etsy cross-references)

## Tag Refresh Schedule

Etsy gives a small boost to listings with refreshed tags every 60-90 days. Set a calendar reminder to:

1. Check Etsy's search suggestions for new trending terms
2. Replace 1-2 underperforming tags with new candidates
3. Update the description to include any new long-tail keywords
4. Save and let Etsy re-index
