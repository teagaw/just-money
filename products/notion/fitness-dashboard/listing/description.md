## Hook Variants for Testing

The first 1-2 sentences are the most important. Etsy shows the first ~150 characters before the "Read more" cutoff. Test these hooks:

**Hook A (Pain-led):**
"🏋️ STOP BOUNCING BETWEEN 5 APPS TO TRACK YOUR FITNESS." *(Current — leads with pain, scores well on emotion)*

**Hook B (Dream-led):**
"✨ Imagine ONE Notion dashboard that tracks every workout, meal, weight, and goal — beautifully." *(Future-pacing, appeals to aspiration)*

**Hook C (Question-led):**
"🤔 What if your workout app, meal tracker, weight log, and goal planner were the SAME app?" *(Curiosity, sets up the answer)*

**Hook D (Specificity-led):**
"📊 8 databases. 12 formulas. 32 views. 1 Notion workspace. Yours for $19." *(Specificity wins on Etsy — buyers love numbers)*

**Recommendation:** Start with Hook A. It scores well on emotional resonance and the "stop doing X" pattern is a proven copywriting formula. Switch to Hook D if you want to lead with the value stack.

## Description Structure Notes

The description follows the **PAS + Stack + CTA** framework:

1. **Pain** (3 paragraphs) — name the problem the buyer is living
2. **Solution intro** (1 paragraph) — name the product
3. **What's inside** (long bulleted list) — the value stack, scannable
4. **Formulas / Technical proof** — credibility through specificity
5. **Works on mobile** — addresses the unspoken "will it work on my phone?" question
6. **Mockup images reference** — directs eyes to the gallery
7. **Bonus list** — the "you also get" creates a perception of extra value
8. **Who it's for / not for** — self-qualification, increases conversion by filtering
9. **How to use** — 10-step setup, makes the buyer feel "I can do this"
10. **Pro tips** — authority positioning, signals depth of thought
11. **The promise** — risk reversal (30-day refund)
12. **Delivery** — sets expectations
13. **Tags** — extra SEO at the bottom of the description
14. **CTA** — clear next action

Every paragraph is doing a job. Nothing is filler.

## Length Note

Etsy descriptions can be up to 10,000 characters. Our full version is ~9,200 characters, which is on the longer side but appropriate for a $19 product. Buyers expect more detail at higher price points.

For a $9 product, cut to the shorter version (~3,500 chars). For a $29 product, expand with more screenshots and testimonials.
