# Budget Pro 2026 — Quick Setup Guide

## What You'll Need
- A Google account
- 30 seconds

## Step 1: Get Your Copy
1. Click this link: **https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1GgtRoP7rVVnW6dzSqa90iGpqT7TDZR1vPTzbFGGodqE/edit?usp=sharing**
2. Click **File > Make a copy** (or press Ctrl+Shift+S)
3. Name it "Budget Pro 2026" (or any name you prefer)
4. Choose your Google Drive folder and click **Make a copy**

## That's it. You're done.

## What You Get
Your copy comes with all 8 tabs already built:
1. **Start Here** — Overview and instructions
2. **Scenario Engine** — Your inputs (9 green cells)
3. **Budget Tracker** — Monthly budget with income/expenses
4. **Goals** — Savings goals with progress tracking
5. **Debt Payoff** — Your debts with snowball vs avalanche comparison
6. **Instructions** — Detailed guide for each tab
7. **Spendable** — What's left after bills
8. **Engine** — Auto-calculated results (don't edit directly)

Pre-filled sample data shows you how it works. Works on phone, tablet, and computer.
