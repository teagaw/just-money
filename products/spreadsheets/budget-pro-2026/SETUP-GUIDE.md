# Budget Pro 2026 — Quick Start

**2 minutes to your debt-free date.**

---

## Step 1: Open your template

Click the **Google Sheet link** in your Etsy download.

→ Budget Pro 2026 opens in a new browser tab. You'll see 8 tabs at the bottom.

📸 *[Screenshot: Browser showing Google Sheet with tabs highlighted at bottom]*

---

## Step 2: Make your own copy

Go to **File → Make a copy**

Name it whatever you want (like "My Budget Pro 2026"). Click **Make a copy**.

→ Now YOU own this sheet. Your numbers, your data, your freedom date.

📸 *[Screenshot: File menu with "Make a copy" highlighted]*

---

## Step 3: Enter your numbers

Click the **Start Here** tab (the first one) to read the quick intro, then click **Scenario Engine** (the second tab) to enter your numbers.

Find the **9 green cells**. These are YOUR numbers:

| What to enter | Example |
|---------------|---------|
| Monthly income | $6,000 |
| Monthly living expenses | $4,000 |
| Current savings | $25,000 |
| Weighted average APR | 7.5% |
| Total debt balance | $28,200 |
| Total monthly debt payment | $790 |
| Inflation adjustment | 3% |
| Custom comparison #1 | $450 |
| Custom comparison #2 | $800 |

📸 *[Screenshot: Scenario Engine with green cells clearly visible]*

**Don't worry about being perfect.** Estimates are fine. You can always change them later.

---

## Step 4: See your freedom date

Look at the **results** — they update instantly:

- **Months to debt-free** — exactly how many months until you're done
- **Your freedom date** — the month and year you make your last payment
- **Interest saved** — how much you save by paying off debt now vs. minimum payments

📸 *[Screenshot: Results section showing "18 months" and a freedom date]*

**That number is real.** It's calculated from YOUR income, YOUR expenses, YOUR debt. Not a guess.

---

## Step 5: Explore the other tabs

Now that you've seen your freedom date, check out the other tabs:

| Tab | What it does |
|-----|-------------|
| **Goals** | Set savings targets and watch your progress bars fill up |
| **Spendable** | See exactly how much you can spend each week and day |
| **Budget Tracker** | Track income vs. expenses across 12 months |
| **Debt Payoff** | Compare snowball vs. avalanche — which strategy saves more? |

📸 *[Screenshot: Grid showing all 5 main tabs with one-line descriptions]*

---

## You're done!

Your spreadsheet is working. Come back anytime to update your numbers and watch your progress.

**Questions?** Check the **Instructions** tab inside the spreadsheet — it has a full FAQ.
