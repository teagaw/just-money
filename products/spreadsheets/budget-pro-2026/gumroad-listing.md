### Know your debt-free date.

Not a vague "someday." A real date on a real calendar.

**Budget Pro 2026** is a Google Sheets spreadsheet that runs scenario simulations on your debt payoff. You enter your debts, your income, your extra payment — and it calculates exactly when you'll be free.

---

### What it does

**Debt Freedom Calculator**
Enter different extra payment amounts and see your debt-free date shift. "What if I pay $50 more per month?" The spreadsheet tells you exactly how many months you save.

**Snowball vs Avalanche — side by side**
Both strategies, month by month, for your specific debts. You see the real dollar difference between the two. No more debating theory.

**Inflation-Adjusted Projections**
Your $500 extra payment today won't have the same purchasing power in 3 years. This spreadsheet accounts for that.

**Spendable Budget Calculator**
See exactly how much money you have left to spend each week — after every bill, every debt payment, every obligation.

**Savings Goals Tracker**
Emergency fund, vacation, car, house — set goals, track progress, see milestones.

**12-Month Budget Tracker**
Monthly income vs. expenses. Category-by-category. Year-over-year comparison.
