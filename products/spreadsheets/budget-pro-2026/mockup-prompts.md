## Production Notes

### Resolution & Format
- Etsy gallery: 2000x2000 px minimum, PNG, sRGB color space
- Hero image: 2700x2025 px, PNG
- Pinterest pins: 1000x1500 px, PNG (vertical crops of mocks 2, 3, 4)
- Instagram carousel: 1080x1080 px JPG, 7 slides
- All mocks: 4K source, no watermarks, no visible AI artifacts

### Style Consistency Rules
- Same color palette across all mocks: midnight navy #0B0F17, emerald #10B981, warm white #F8FAFC, gold #F59E0B
- Same typography mood: clean modern sans-serif (Inter, SF Pro, or Montserrat family)
- Same lighting: soft natural, golden hour or warm white, never harsh
- No emojis, no cartoon icons, no stock-photo smiles
- Charts must have accurate, readable numbers
- Depth of field in lifestyle shots: subject sharp, background softly blurred
- No visible brand logos except the product's own title

### Workflow
1. Generate 3-4 variations of each prompt in Midjourney
2. Upscale the best with Midjourney's "U" command or Magnific AI
3. Touch up in Photoshop or Affinity Photo: correct any text errors, adjust contrast, add subtle grain if needed
4. Run a final pass to ensure color consistency across the full set
5. Export at the required sizes and formats
6. Archive raw generations in `assets/raw/` for re-edits
