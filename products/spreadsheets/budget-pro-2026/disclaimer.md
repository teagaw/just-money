## 3. Assumptions

This spreadsheet relies on the following assumptions. If any do not match your situation, results will be inaccurate:

- Monthly payments remain **consistent** throughout the payoff period
- No payments are skipped or missed
- Interest compounds **monthly**
- Inflation projections use a fixed annual rate
- Income and expenses remain relatively stable unless manually updated
