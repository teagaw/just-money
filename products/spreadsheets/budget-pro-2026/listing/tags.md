## How Tags Were Selected

### Research Process
1. **Primary keyword:** "budget spreadsheet" — searched directly, highest match to product
2. **Platform anchor:** "google sheets" — required for Sheets-based products
3. **Outcome keywords:** "debt tracker", "net worth tracker", "savings goal" — what buyers search when they have a specific problem
4. **Use-case keywords:** "personal finance", "financial planner" — broader intent signals
5. **Action keywords:** "budget template", "monthly budget" — what buyers are ready to download

### Volume Tiers
- **High volume (4 tags):** "budget spreadsheet", "google sheets", "monthly budget", "budget planner" — these get 1,000+ Etsy searches/month
- **Medium volume (6 tags):** "budget template", "debt tracker", "net worth tracker", "savings goal", "expense tracker", "financial planner" — 200-1,000 searches/month
- **Low-Medium (3 tags):** "personal finance", "money dashboard", "debt payoff plan" — 50-200 searches/month but lower competition

### Strategy
- Tags 1-3 are the highest-volume primary terms
- Tags 4-7 capture intent variations
- Tags 8-13 are problem-aware and feature-aware (helps when buyer searches for a specific outcome like "debt payoff")

## What Was Avoided

- **Single words:** "budget", "money", "finance" — too generic, wasted on broad matches
- **"Best"/"top":** Etsy doesn't index superlatives for search
- **"2026":** Year-stamped tags decay in value after 2026
- **"#":** Etsy doesn't read hashtags
- **"Template printable":** This is a digital product, not printable — different intent
- **"Google Sheets template":** Redundant with "google sheets" + "budget template"

## Refresh Schedule
- Re-evaluate tags every 6 months
- Drop tags whose conversion rate is < 0.5% after 90 days
- Test adding 1-2 trending terms (e.g., "AI budget", "couples budget") in Q4

## How Etsy Uses Tags
- Etsy matches tags against buyer search queries
- Multi-word phrases match exactly (no partial matching within a tag)
- The 13 tags are the maximum Etsy allows per listing
- Tags are case-insensitive
- Tags work alongside the title and category for ranking
