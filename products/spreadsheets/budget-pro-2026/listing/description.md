## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to be good at spreadsheets?**
A: No. The tutorial tab walks you through everything. If you can use a phone, you can use this.

**Q: Can I use this in Excel or Apple Numbers?**
A: Google Sheets is the primary platform and works perfectly. Excel 365 and Apple Numbers work too — just a few formulas use QUERY() which is Sheets-specific. Documented in the setup guide.

**Q: Can I share it with my partner?**
A: Yes. Share the file like any other Google Sheet. Both of you can enter transactions in real time.

**Q: Can I customize the colors and categories?**
A: Yes, fully. The Categories tab controls colors, monthly defaults, and tax flags. The Dashboard color tokens are listed in the design spec file.

**Q: What if I have more than 25 spending categories?**
A: Add as many rows as you want to the Budget vs Actual tab. The formulas auto-extend.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: It's a Google Sheet, so the Google Sheets mobile app is your app. Free, fast, syncs to your phone.

**Q: How does the debt snowball work?**
A: Sort your debts smallest-to-largest. Pay the minimum on all. Throw every extra dollar at the smallest. When it's paid off, roll that payment into the next-smallest. The Debt Payoff tab does the math for you.

**Q: Can I import my bank transactions?**
A: Yes. Export your bank's CSV (most banks have this). Copy/paste into the Transactions tab. The Category column is a dropdown with auto-fill rules.
