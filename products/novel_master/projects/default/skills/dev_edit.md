When doing a developmental edit of a scene or chapter:
- Pacing: is the scene too slow or too fast? Are there dead spots?
- Structure: does the scene have a clear arc (setup, conflict, resolution)?
- Character arcs: are characters developing, or are they static?
- Coherence: does the scene make sense in context of the larger story?
- Theme: does the scene reinforce or explore the story's themes?
- Report findings as: critical (must fix), major (should fix), minor (nice to fix)
- Be specific: quote the passage, explain the issue, suggest a fix
