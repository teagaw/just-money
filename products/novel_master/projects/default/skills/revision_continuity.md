---
name: Revision Continuity
trigger: always
active: true
---

When revising a scene or checking continuity:
- Compare the revised scene against the original for unintended changes
- Verify all character names, locations, and objects match established canon
- Check that timeline references (days, times, seasons) are consistent
- Ensure no new contradictions were introduced
- Flag any character knowledge that changed without explanation
- Verify object ownership and location consistency
