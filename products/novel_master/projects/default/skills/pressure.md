When stress-testing for narrative weakness:
- Predictability: can the reader guess what happens next? If yes, add a twist or complication.
- Stakes: are the consequences real? Does failure matter?
- Tension: is there pressure building, or does the scene feel flat?
- Escalation: does each scene raise the stakes or narrow options?
- Absent consequence: do actions have results, or do they disappear?
- Low conflict: is there genuine opposition, or is the protagonist unchallenged?
- Earned resolution: are solutions set up earlier, or do they come from nowhere?
