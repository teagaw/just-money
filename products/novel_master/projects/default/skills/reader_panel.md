When simulating reader reactions to a scene:
- Read as a first-time reader with no prior knowledge
- Note moments of confusion, boredom, or disengagement
- Identify what works: hooks, tension, emotional beats, surprises
- Flag info-dumps or exposition that breaks immersion
- Check if character motivations are clear to a new reader
- Rate pacing: too slow, just right, or too rushed
- Note any questions raised that aren't answered by scene end
