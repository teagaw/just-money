name: Scene Diagnosis
trigger: always
active: true
---

When diagnosing a scene that isn't working:
- Goal: what does the POV character want in this scene?
- Conflict: what opposes the goal? (internal, external, or both)
- Outcome: does the scene end with the goal achieved, failed, or complicated?
- If any of the above is missing, the scene may lack dramatic structure.
- Check: does the scene change the situation? If the world is the same at the end, the scene may be filler.
- Check: is the scene in the right place? Could it be cut, moved, or merged?
