When writing or reviewing worldbuilding:
- Locations: are they distinct and memorable? Do they have sensory details?
- Systems (magic, tech, social): are the rules consistent and clear?
- Factions: do they have clear motivations and conflicts?
- Invented terms: are they introduced naturally, not info-dumped?
- World rules: never contradict established lore
- Culture: does it feel lived-in, not just described?
- Avoid: info-dumps, exposition-heavy dialogue, "as you know" conversations
