---
name: World Consistency
trigger: always
active: true
---

When checking world-building consistency:
- Are locations consistent with their established descriptions?
- Do systems (magic, tech, social) follow their established rules?
- Are factions' motivations and conflicts consistent?
- Do invented terms have consistent usage and meaning?
- Are cultural details consistent across scenes?
- Avoid: contradicting established world rules, changing location details, inconsistent technology levels
