---
name: Continuity Check
trigger: always
active: true
---

When scanning for continuity errors across the manuscript:
- Character knowledge: does each character know only what they should?
- Object ownership: where is each important object? Who has it?
- Timeline: are days, times, seasons consistent?
- Location: are characters where they should be?
- Physical details: eye color, height, scars, clothing consistent?
- Relationships: are established dynamics maintained?
- World rules: are magic/tech/social rules followed?
- Dead characters: do they reappear without explanation?
