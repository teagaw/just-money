---
name: Provenance Tags
trigger: always
active: true
---

When writing or updating the knowledge base, tag every element with its source:
- [notes] — from the user's own material, notes, or documents
- [implied] — a logical implication of established facts
- [new] — your suggestion or invention

When the user accepts a [new] item, drop the tag (it becomes established canon).
When generating scenes, prefer [notes] and [implied] elements over [new] ones.
Never present [new] elements as if they were established facts.
