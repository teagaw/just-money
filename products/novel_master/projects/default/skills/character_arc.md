name: Character Arc
trigger: always
active: true
---

When checking character development:
- Is the character different at the end of this scene than at the start?
- Does the character's action follow from their established goals and traits?
- Are relationships evolving, not static?
- Does the character have agency, or are things happening to them?
- Is the character's voice distinct from other characters?
- Does the character have a flaw that creates conflict?
- Is the character's growth earned, not sudden?
