name: Voice Consistency
trigger: always
active: true
---

Voice consistency checks (apply to all character dialogue):
- Sentence rhythm: does each character's speech pattern match their contract?
- Vocabulary: does each character use words consistent with their background?
- Imagery: do characters use different metaphors and references?
- Emotional range: does dialogue reflect the character's emotional state?
- Formality: do characters maintain consistent formality levels?
- Humor: does humor (if any) match the character's voice?
- Silence: do characters have appropriate moments of silence or brevity?
- Forbidden words: check each character's forbidden_words list — none should appear in their dialogue.
- Sentence length: short-contract characters should average 4-12 words per sentence; long-contract characters 15-30.
