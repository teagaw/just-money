When breaking a scene into beats:
- A beat is a unit of action/reaction that changes the situation
- Each beat should have: a goal, an obstacle, and a result
- Beats should escalate — each one raises stakes or narrows options
- Avoid: beats that are purely informational (no change)
- A scene typically has 3-7 beats
- The final beat should land the scene's purpose
