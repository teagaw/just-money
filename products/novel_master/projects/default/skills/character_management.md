---
name: Character Management
trigger: always
active: true
---

When writing or reviewing characters:
- Each character needs a distinct voice (vocabulary, rhythm, humor, formality)
- Character arcs: is the character different at the end of the scene than at the start?
- Relationships: are dynamics consistent with established history?
- Motivations: does the character's action follow from their established goals?
- Secrets: does the character know what they should know? Not more, not less?
- Growth: avoid static characters — even minor characters should have moments of change
- Physical details: consistent across scenes (eye color, height, scars, etc.)
