When analyzing the overall story:
- Character arc tracker: for each character, list key moments of change across scenes
- Plot thread map: list all active threads, their status (open/resolved/abandoned), and which scenes they appear in
- Three-act structure: identify setup, confrontation, resolution beats
- Check for: missing scenes, unresolved setups, dropped threads, pacing issues
- Identify the story's spine: the central conflict that drives everything
- Flag scenes that don't advance plot or character
