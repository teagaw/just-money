When developing character relationships:
- What does each character want from the other?
- What power dynamic exists between them?
- How has the relationship changed since they first met?
- What do they know about each other that the other doesn't?
- What's the unspoken tension or subtext?
- Avoid: relationships that are static or purely functional
- Conflict in relationships should escalate, not repeat
