When researching for worldbuilding or factual grounding:
- Verify historical details (dates, names, events, technology)
- Check cultural accuracy (customs, language, social norms)
- Confirm scientific/medical/technical details
- Mark uncertain facts with [verify] in the text
- Prefer specific details over generic ones
- Avoid anachronisms (words, concepts, or objects from the wrong era)
- Label anything from general knowledge as unvetted
