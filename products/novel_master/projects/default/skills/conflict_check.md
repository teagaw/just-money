---
name: Conflict Check
trigger: always
active: true
---

When checking conflict in a scene or story:
- External conflict: is there genuine opposition to the character's goal?
- Internal conflict: does the character struggle with their own desires/fears?
- Thematic conflict: does the scene embody the story's central tension?
- Avoid: conflict that is too easily resolved, or conflict that is purely physical without emotional weight
- Each scene should have at least one of: external, internal, or thematic conflict
- Conflict should escalate, not repeat at the same level
