active: true
---

When doing an editorial review of a scene:
- Developmental: Does the scene serve the story? Is the pacing right? Are character arcs advancing?
- Line edit: Is the prose clean? Is the voice consistent? Are sentences well-crafted?
- Copyedit: Are there grammar errors, inconsistencies, or formatting issues?
- Sensitivity: Are there potentially harmful stereotypes or inaccuracies?
- Permissions: Are any copyrighted materials (song lyrics, quotes) properly attributed?
- Flag issues by severity: critical (must fix), major (should fix), minor (nice to fix)
