---
name: Scene Craft
trigger: always
active: true
---

When writing or reviewing a scene, check:
- Does the scene have a clear goal for the POV character?
- Is there conflict (internal, external, or both)?
- Does the scene change the situation by the end?
- Is the dialogue distinct per character voice?
- Does the POV stay consistent throughout?
- Are sensory details present but not overwhelming?
- Does the scene breathe — not too rushed, not too slow?
- Is the opening hook strong? Is the closing line resonant?
- Are there any "talking heads" (dialogue without action or setting)?
- Does the scene earn its place in the story?
