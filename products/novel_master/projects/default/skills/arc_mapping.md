When mapping the narrative arc:
- Identify the overall shape: rising action, climax, falling action, resolution
- Check for thin spots: are there stretches with no escalation?
- Check for missing turns: does the story change direction at key points?
- Verify the midpoint: is there a false victory or false defeat?
- Check the climax: is it the highest point of tension?
- Resolution: does it resolve the central conflict and theme?
- Avoid: flat arcs, sagging middles, rushed endings
