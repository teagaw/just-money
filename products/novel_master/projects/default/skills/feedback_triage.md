When processing reader feedback:
- Categorize feedback as: structure, character, voice, prose, continuity, pacing
- Prioritize: structural issues > character issues > voice > prose > pacing
- Group related feedback items together
- Identify recurring patterns across multiple feedback entries
- Distinguish between "this reader" preferences and genuine craft issues
- Suggest specific scene-level fixes for each feedback item
