When generating or updating a book bible from scenes:
- Characters: name, role, physical description, voice, motivations, secrets, relationships
- Locations: name, description, sensory details, significance to story
- Timeline: key events in chronological order with scene references
- World rules: magic systems, technology, social norms, hard constraints
- Open threads: unresolved questions, planted promises, active conflicts
- Relationships: who knows what, who trusts whom, power dynamics
- Update the bible after each accepted scene — don't let it go stale
