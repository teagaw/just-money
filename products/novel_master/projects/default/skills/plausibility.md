---
name: Plausibility
trigger: always
active: true
---

When stress-testing a scene's internal logic:
- Does the plot make sense? Could events actually happen this way?
- Are character decisions believable given their established traits?
- Are there logical contradictions or impossible situations?
- Does the world's internal logic hold (magic rules, tech limits, social norms)?
- Would a skeptical reader find the premise credible?
- Are coincidences used to resolve plot? (Flag these)
- Does cause follow from effect, or does the plot drive the characters?
