Rain drummed the canvas awnings. Puddles stretched across the Salt Market stones. Maren stopped at the rope-seller's stall. The old man's hands were a blur of knots. He paused, rope coiled halfway. She stood in a puddle. "Door." Just the word. No please. No I need.

The old man's eyes flicked to the sealed writ at her belt, then back to her face. "Doors cost."

"I have marks."
