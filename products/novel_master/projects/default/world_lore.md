# World Lore

## Setting
A rain-soaked port city called Duskport, ruled by a merchant council. Magic exists but is rare, feared, and regulated by the Wardens.

## Magic rules
- Only "marked" individuals can cast. Casting drains stamina; overuse kills.
- Wardens hunt unlicensed casters.
- No resurrection, no mind control, no teleportation.

## Geography
- Duskport: harbor, old quarter, warden barracks, the Salt Market.
- Surrounding: the Ashen Reach (wasteland), the Tide Isles (smuggler haven).

## Factions
- Merchant Council: money, order, corrupt.
- Wardens: law, fear, zealots.
- The Unmarked: unlicensed casters, hidden, hunted.

## Currency
- Silver marks (common), gold crowns (rare). No paper money.

## Tone
Grim, grounded, slow-burn. Short sentences. No purple prose. Violence has consequences.
