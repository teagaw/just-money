# World Lore — The Leaky Secret

## Setting
A port city called Duskport. Magic is rare and feared. Wardens hunt unlicensed casters.

## Magic rules
- Only "marked" individuals can cast. Casting drains stamina.
- Wardens hunt unlicensed casters. No resurrection, no mind control.

## Factions
- Wardens: law, fear, zealots.
- Merchant Council: money, order, corrupt.

## Tone
Grim, grounded, slow-burn. Short sentences. No purple prose.
