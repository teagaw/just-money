# Just Money Brand — Domain & Social Media Handle Research
**Date:** June 13, 2026  
**Researcher:** Business Strategist

---

## Executive Summary

The "Just Money" brand faces significant challenges in securing the ideal digital identity. The primary domain (justmoney.com) is registered but expiring in July 2025, while a major competitor (JustMoney.co.za) has established presence across multiple platforms. However, several high-value alternatives remain available, particularly on X/Twitter and YouTube.

**Key Finding:** The .com domain is taken but may be available for acquisition. The .co and .app alternatives are strong options for a fintech brand targeting millennials/Gen Z.

#### Registrar Recommendations

**Best Value: Cloudflare Registrar**
- .com: $10.44/year (no markup, at-cost pricing)
- Free WHOIS privacy
- Free SSL certificates
- Best for long-term domain holders

**Alternative: Porkbun**
- Competitive .co and .app pricing
- Free WHOIS privacy
- Good for multiple TLDs

**Avoid: GoDaddy**
- High renewal prices ($21.99 for .com vs $10.44 at Cloudflare)
- Aggressive upsells ($9.99 domain → $150+ without careful unchecking)
- WHOIS privacy costs $9.99/year (free everywhere else)
